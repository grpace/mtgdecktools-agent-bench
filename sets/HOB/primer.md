### **The Hobbit (HOB) Limited Context**
**HOB supplement only.** The main prompt already defines your card pool and pool-only rules. Use **your own card evaluation for raw power** and this section for **lane fit, density, curve, mana, and how cards work together in the 40**. [[Card names]] are examples of what to look for in your pool—not cards to add. **Build the best 40 from the actual pool.** Brief gameplay notes appear only where they explain why a card earns a slot or how a lane wins (useful for primers).

**Overrides generic Limited defaults:** Fast format—prioritize 1–2 drops over late value. BR can run **~10–12 creatures** because [[Goblin Plate Mail]], [[Rage into the Valley]], and amass spells count as threats; most lanes want **~15–17**. Menace is common—Recruit tokens and small bodies are worth extra slots in WU/WB/UB. **Taplands split by speed:** BR/BG/RW want **~1** on-color dual or [[Hobbit Hole]] when one-drops matter; WU/UB/GU can run **2–3** because they curve higher and care less about turn-one plays. WU and off-color blue pairs want **~4–6 removal** combined—blue answers alone are weak stand-ins.

**Compare from the pool, not the published order.** Start from the deepest color you actually have. Black-deep: BR vs BG (and UB/WB if blue or white payoffs plus black removal). White-deep: RW vs WU vs WB. Blue-deep: WU vs UB vs UR (and GU if green landfall payoffs are open). Pick the 40 with the best mix of curve, interaction, and castable power—not the lane you hoped for at pick one.

### **Format Basics**

* **Five supported lanes:** WU Recruit, BR Amass, RW Storied, BG Ferocious, GU Landfall. Commons mostly signal one home. When two lanes share a color, take the one with the better mix of gold, density, answers, and curve.
* **Aggressive vs value shells:** BR, BG, and RW are **curve-out decks**—mana efficiency turns 1–5 wins races. WU and UB are **value decks**—draw-twice and Recruit scale if they survive. **GU is mid-range:** landfall triggers can race early or grind late, but it is not a draw-control shell like WU/UB. Build to your lane's speed, especially on lands.
* **Off-color blue pairs are real options:** UB, UR, and WB are not signposted but can be the best 40 when blue is deep and the second color adds what blue lacks (black removal/equipment, red two-drops and burn, white Recruit density). GU is the supported landfall lane—usually **green-heavy** with a few blue answers, not a blue control deck. Enter an off-color lane when you have **payoffs + enablers + fixing** for that pair—not because you opened one gold bomb.
* **Power order:** **BR** has the highest average baseline; **WU and UB** beat that when their full packages come together (draw-twice + Recruit or + black removal). BG and RW are solid mid-tier. **GU** is weakest (~low-50s%) unless the pool has real landfall payoffs and early bodies—not a long grind. Black commons are deep; a thin BR pile loses to a coherent WU or UB 40.
* **Raw power vs this format:** Vacuum card ratings miss roles. [[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Goblin Plate Mail]], [[Gollum, Silent Slinker]], [[Desolation Prowler]], [[Ravening Warg]], [[Lakeshore Apothecary]], [[Patient Instructor]], and [[Dwarven Mattock]] punch above their look. [[Wood Elves]] is strong in GU, weak in BG. [[Sting, Bilbo's Sword]] and [[Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver]] often lose to cheaper Equipment. Gold signposts besides [[Bard the Bowman]] are playable filler, not auto-picks—[[Bolg of the North]] and [[The Chief Warg]] lose to cheaper on-lane cards. [[Gandalf, Spark Starter]] and [[Gandalf, Wandering Wizard]] are opposites: Spark Starter is undervalued top-end; Wandering Wizard underperforms in blue shells.
* **Blue is strong but not control:** Equipment and menace punish slow "answer everything" plans. Blue wins via card flow—[[Plunder the Trollshaws]] flashback, [[Bilbo, Luckwearer]], and draw-twice payoffs scale without a dedicated finisher. Skip [[Gandalf, Wandering Wizard]] unless it is clearly among your best five pool cards.
* **Two-color default.** Splash a third color when that card is one of the best in the 40 and you can cast it (on-color duals / [[Hobbit Hole]] plus a splash basic). **White bombs belong in WB, not as a BR splash**—double-white rares punish off-color mana. Hybrids ([[Goblin Plate Mail]], [[Patient Instructor]], [[Duskwatch Hunter]], [[Large Bear]], [[Mirkwood Nurturer]]) glue neighbor pairs and off-color shells.
* **Mechanic + type:** Commons/uncommons are mechanic-first; typal peaks at Company rares ([[Bard's Company]], [[Chief Warg's Company]], [[Thranduil's Company]], [[Dáin's Company]], [[Bolg's Company]]). Each pair has a tapped dual that can sac for type-specific +1/+1 counters—prefer on-color; aggressive lanes limit to **one** copy.
* **Adventures / Sagas / legends:** Prefer Adventures where either half is playable. Sagas are often strong and count toward Storied. Legendary density is high—**two copies of a legend is usually fine**; three only for a clear B-tier or better bomb ([[Bilbo Baggins, Burglar]] can adventure away extras). Treasure is thin.
* **Fixing:** On-color duals first. [[Hobbit Hole]] is best in UB/GU (landfall triggers, Halfling density); one copy is fine in BR when fetching a Golem matters. [[Thrór's Map]] underperforms in BR/BG/RW. [[The Lonely Mountain]] is excellent in BR/RW mirror races—free creature when flooded. [[Lake-town]] rewards white splashes in GU; avoid splashing **blue into RW**. [[Troop of Ponies]], [[Long-Bodied Grey Dog]], [[Giant's Boulder]], and [[Old Thrush]] are sideboard/slow-shell cards—[[Old Thrush]] is filler in WU/UB/GU, bad in BR/BG/RW.
* **Typical composition:** ~15–17 creatures, 5–6 interaction, 17 lands. Count Amass Army, Recruit tokens, and Equipment that makes a body ([[Goblin Plate Mail]]) toward creature count and curve. Heavy blue draw-twice decks: most non-removal slots are **enablers + payoffs**; ~5–6 removal total.

### **Key Mechanics** (deckbuilding lens)

1. **Recruit (WU):** Draw, discard; nonland discard → 1/1 Human Soldier. **Recruit is its own engine**—not just a draw-two helper. Include Recruit sources, draw-twice payoffs, and graveyard synergy ([[Plunder the Trollshaws]], [[Eagle's Rescue]], [[Moment of Glory]]). Tokens count as early board. [[Long Lake Nuisance]] Recruits as a flyer. [[The Mountain-king's Return]] Recruits then reanimates MV≤3—pairs with [[Mirkwood Nurturer]] for recursion. After stabilizing on low life, prioritize lifelink ([[Bard the Bowman]], [[Master's Councillors]] counters) to close.
2. **Draw-twice package (all blue pairs):** [[Lakeshore Apothecary]] and [[Master's Councillors]] are the core payoffs—trigger whenever you draw your second card each turn. Strongest in WU and UB; weaker in GU unless the shell is draw-heavy. Enablers: [[Plunder the Trollshaws]], [[Bilbo, Luckwearer]], [[Patient Instructor]], [[Long Lake Nuisance]], [[Bilbo Baggins, Burglar]], [[Reverent Howl]], [[Confusticate and Bebother]]. **More payoffs → more enablers**; first [[Lakeshore Apothecary]] is weak if you cannot trigger it most turns. [[Master's Councillors]]: grow and beat down; mill is incidental—mill yourself when graveyards are close (you want [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] in the yard, not their flashback). [[Bard the Bowman]] adds lifelink counters in WU/UB.
3. **Amass (BR):** Grow one Goblin Army. Build around noncreature spells + [[Bothersome Noisemaker]], [[Rage into the Valley]], and sagas—not a sacrifice-grind default. Backup sac package ([[Snowslope Hunter]], [[Stir Up Trouble]]) only when fodder is already plentiful. Prefer attacking bodies ([[Front Porch Sentries]]) over fodder commons ([[Goblin-town Flunkies]]). Best curve: one-drop → equip [[Goblin Plate Mail]] or two-drop spell into Army.
4. **Storied (RW):** Storied permanent + 3+ artifacts/legends/Sagas → enduring story for the rest of the game. Equipment and legends fill the deck naturally—story usually comes free. [[Dori, Bearer of Friends]] (3/2 trample, Treasure) helps hit the count fast. [[Thorin Oakenshield]] is an equip carrier, not a solo build-around. [[Dáin Ironfoot]] and [[Fíli the Pathfinder]] / [[Kíli the Resourceful]] are the real Dwarf payoffs.
5. **Ferocious (BG):** Needs power 4+ on board; Ferocious cards count themselves. Core sequencing: [[Nasty Little Rabbit]] / [[Nighthowl Pursuer]] into [[Desolation Prowler]]—not [[Wood Elves]] ramp. [[Duskwatch Hunter]] ETB counter enables the curve. [[The Chief Warg]] draws under ferocious but cheaper Wolves usually outpace it. [[Beorn the Fierce]] shifts the deck toward Bears; otherwise Wolves are default bodies.
6. **Landfall (GU):** One plan—**green-heavy landfall stompy** with blue answers, not slow ramp or a critical-mass Elf pile. Core: twos, [[Mirkwood Pathmaker]], [[Silvan Reveler]], [[Wood Elves]] (extra land-drop triggers), [[Hobbit Hole]], closers like [[Down in the Valley]] and [[Thranduil's Company]]. [[Patient Instructor]] is a top card here—often underdrafted. **Elf layer:** add [[Woodland Weavemaster]] only when you already have **~6+ strong Elves** in the pool; it is unplayable in BG and not worth forcing with weak blue Elves. [[Mirkwood Nurturer]] can bounce lands for extra triggers in a landfall-heavy GU list; in UB/WU it loops Equipment and graveyard value—it is **not** a standalone landfall engine.
7. **Adventures / Equipment:** Flexible Adventure halves preferred. [[Gollum, Silent Slinker]]—two copies are usually great in WB/UB; fine in BR. Equipment anchors RW, WB, UB, and black attrition: [[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Goblin Plate Mail]], [[Dwarven Mattock]]. [[Sting, Bilbo's Sword]] and [[Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver]] are expensive; cheaper Equipment usually wins the slot.

### **Draft Archetypes**

Each supported lane: **dual**, then **gold signpost**. Count enablers vs payoffs; drop a payoff if the pool lacks density.

* **WU Humans / Recruit** — dual [[Lake-town]]; signpost [[Bard the Bowman]]
  * **Deck shape:** Recruit + selection + flyers/tall threats—not a control deck. Draw-twice payoffs ([[Lakeshore Apothecary]], [[Master's Councillors]], [[Bard the Bowman]]) plus Recruit; most slots are enablers, payoffs, or removal. Curve sits at 2–4; need **6–7 two-drops** in this fast format. Can run **2–3** [[Lake-town]] / [[Hobbit Hole]] because WU lacks good one-drops.
  * **Core includes:** [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] (top common here), [[Lakeshore Apothecary]], **~4 removal** ([[Magnificent End]] best common—better here than in RW/BR; mix [[Uneasy Partings]] / [[Enchanted River's Grasp]]). Recruit bodies: [[Long Lake Nuisance]], [[Bilbo Baggins, Burglar]], [[Patient Instructor]]—duplicate Bilbos are fine. [[Dwarven Provisioner]] goes wide here (underperforms in RW).
  * **Key uncommons:** [[Celebrate the Mountain-king]] (exiles artifacts/enchantments; strong with [[Plunder the Trollshaws]]). [[The Mountain-king's Return]] (Recruit + reanimate MV≤3). [[Stone by Sunlight]], [[Bilbo, Luckwearer]], [[Thranduil, Sindarin Liege]], [[Iron Hills Blacksmith]] + [[Eagle's Rescue]]. [[Esgaroth Garrison]] is the best WU curve-topper—Recruits on ETB and scales with go-wide tokens.
  * **Recruit-graveyard slots:** [[Eagle's Rescue]] and [[Moment of Glory]] need **~6+ Recruit sources**—awkward as on-curve auras without fodder. [[Lake-town Toymaker]] is filler payoff; better when Recruit-heavy, not when instant-speed draw ([[Bilbo, Luckwearer]], [[Great Gilded Boat]]) dominates.
  * **Dwarf sub-theme:** [[Fíli the Pathfinder]] and [[Kíli the Resourceful]] raise [[Dwarven Provisioner]] and [[Bofur, Reliable Guardian]] when those cards are already in the 40.
  * **Mana:** 17 lands default. **16 lands** only when most draw triggers are Recruit (not [[Plunder the Trollshaws]]-heavy)—exception, not rule.
  * **When to pick this over UB:** White Recruit density, [[Celebrate the Mountain-king]], [[The Mountain-king's Return]], or [[Bard's Company]]—not just because blue is open.

* **BR Goblins / Amass** — dual [[Goblin-town]]; signpost [[Bolg of the North]]
  * **Deck shape:** Low curve, spells into one Army, menace pressure. [[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Goblin Plate Mail]] (first copy best), [[Ravening Warg]] anchor the black/red core; [[Gollum, Silent Slinker]] is fine here but rates higher in WB/UB. **~1 tapland max**—curve out turns 1–5; using all mana early wins ~70%+ on the play.
  * **Spells / uncommons:** [[Rage into the Valley]] (counts as a creature slot), [[Tidings of War]] (one copy usually enough), [[Pinecone Strike]], [[Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern]], [[Down, Down to Goblin-town]], [[Misty Mountains Raider]]. [[Gandalf, Spark Starter]] is undervalued top-end; [[Smaug, the Great Calamity]] adventure is fine filler, not a build-around.
  * **Density:** Spells + Army enablers. Prefer attacking bodies over fodder. [[Bolg of the North]] is awkward in most lists—cheaper BR cards usually do more. [[Bolg's Company]] when Goblins are already dense. [[The Lonely Mountain]] is premium fixing here. Sacrifice package only with extra fodder. Do not splash for off-color bombs.

* **RW Dwarves / Storied** — dual [[Iron Hills]]; signpost [[Thorin Oakenshield]]
  * **Deck shape:** Equipment + Dwarf legends; high creature count for carriers. Storied turns on from normal deck construction. Best curve: turn-one Dwarf → turn-two [[Dwarven Mattock]] or [[Goblin Plate Mail]]. **Limit [[Iron Hills]] to one copy** when running one-drops.
  * **Equipment package:** [[Goblin Plate Mail]], [[Dwarven Mattock]], [[Ragged Short Spear]], [[Well-Worn Spatula]]. [[Dwarven Shortsword]]—one copy usually enough. [[Sting, Bilbo's Sword]] / [[Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver]] lose to cheaper Equipment.
  * **Support:** [[Iron Hills Blacksmith]], [[Bothersome Noisemaker]], [[Glóin the Mighty]], [[Bifur, Melodic Rider]], [[Dori, Bearer of Friends]]. Rares: [[Dáin Ironfoot]], [[Dwalin, Weaponmaster]], [[Dáin's Company]]. [[Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills]] is overpicked—needs ~6–8 Dwarves; prefer [[Dáin Ironfoot]] when the pool is already Dwarf-heavy.
  * **Density:** [[Dwarven Mauler]] wants ~4–5 Equipment. [[An Unexpected Party]] for Dwarf tokens + anthem. Keep enough bodies to hold gear. Do not splash blue—blue high-rarity cards do not carry RW.

* **BG Wolves / Ferocious** — dual [[Mirkwood]]; signpost [[The Chief Warg]]
  * **Deck shape:** Black beatdown package plus green 1–2 drops and reach. Curve Rabbit/Pursuer into [[Desolation Prowler]]—lowest curve in the format. **~1 tapland**; skip [[Wood Elves]] for the ferocious curve.
  * **Green includes:** [[Attercop]], [[Wargling]], [[Duskwatch Hunter]], [[Mirkwood Pathmaker]]. Bears with [[Beorn the Fierce]]; otherwise Wolves ([[Little Bear]], [[Ordinary Bear]], [[Gigantic Big Bear]]).
  * **Interaction:** [[Reverent Howl]], [[Warg Tactics]].
  * **Density:** ~**8 early enablers** (cheap power-4, +2 Equipment, ETB counters) before leaning on Ferocious payoffs. Closers: [[Head of the Hunt]], [[Chief Warg's Company]] on curve. [[The Chief Warg]] is playable but often outpaced by cheaper lane cards—do not splash it into BR.

* **GU Elves / Landfall** — dual [[Elvenking's Halls]]; signpost [[Silvan Reveler]]
  * **Deck shape:** Green-heavy landfall stompy + blue answers—not control, not slow ramp. [[Mirkwood Pathmaker]], [[Silvan Reveler]], [[Beorn's Hospitality]], [[Down in the Valley]], and [[Thranduil's Company]] are the lane payoffs; [[Silvan Reveler]] needs real landfall density—treat it as a payoff check, not an auto-include. [[Patient Instructor]] and [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] fill the blue slots. **~2–3** taplands OK.
  * **Elf upgrade:** When [[Woodland Weavemaster]] is in the pool with **~6+ strong Elves** ([[Wood Elves]], [[Patient Instructor]], solid green Elves), slot Weavemaster as the engine—still landfall-backed, not a separate archetype. Without that density, skip Weavemaster and stay on the landfall core.
  * **Landfall enablers:** [[Wood Elves]], [[Hobbit Hole]] (double-trigger potential), [[Attercop]]. Use [[Mirkwood Nurturer]] only when land-bounce is part of a real plan. Splash white ([[Lake-town]], [[Magnificent End]]) when blue removal is thin.

**Off-color archetypes (when the pool supports them):**

* **UB — draw-two goodstuff** — no signpost; pivot when blue **and** black are deep with removal + draw-twice pieces
  * **When it makes sense:** Early [[Lakeshore Apothecary]] / [[Master's Councillors]] plus black interaction ([[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Ravening Warg]], [[Gollum, Silent Slinker]])—often the best blue shell when white is thin. Blue commons + black removal is the core.
  * **Deck shape:** Same draw-twice core as WU minus white Recruit density—more black removal and equipment. ~5–6 removal; enablers + payoffs fill most other slots. Not a slow control deck.
  * **Key synergies:** [[Mirkwood Nurturer]] + [[Crude Bent Blade]] (pick up the Blade, opponent sacrifices again). [[Stony-Voiced Goblins]] curves into [[Crude Bent Blade]]. [[Rage into the Valley]] and [[Reverent Howl]] trigger draw-twos; [[Reverent Howl]] lifelink on a big [[Lakeshore Apothecary]] wins races. [[The Mountain-king's Return]] + [[Mirkwood Nurturer]] loops value.
  * **Fixing / density:** [[Hobbit Hole]] is strong—most Halflings are U or B. [[Most Decrepit Old Bird // Speak Secrets]] wants **~9+ instants/sorceries** in the 40; mill [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] yourself. [[Old Fat Spider Can't See Me]] only with 2–3 [[Master's Councillors]].

* **UR — spells tempo** — no signpost; pivot when blue payoffs meet red two-drops and burn
  * **When it makes sense:** Blue opened with [[Lakeshore Apothecary]] / [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] but white is contested or weak; red adds [[Pinecone Strike]], [[Bothersome Noisemaker]], and [[Óin the Brave]] where blue lacks two-drops and removal.
  * **Deck shape:** Draw-twice payoffs + cheap red interaction + noncreature spells. [[Bothersome Noisemaker]] amasses on every spell—exceptional here. [[Óin the Brave]] rummages into draw-twice turns; discard [[Plunder the Trollshaws]] to rummage for one mana saved vs flashback.
  * **Density:** Blue enabler/payoff core plus red spells—[[Bothersome Noisemaker]] and draw-twice payoffs carry the plan, not red burn alone.

* **WB — equipment Recruit** — no signpost; pivot when white Recruit/rares meet black equipment
  * **When it makes sense:** White rares ([[An Unexpected Party]], [[Fíli the Pathfinder]], [[Kíli the Resourceful]], [[Settle the Wreckage]], [[The Eagles Are Coming!]]) plus black equipment ([[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Goblin Plate Mail]]) and Recruit tokens to equip or sacrifice. **Prefer WB over splashing those bombs into BR.** [[Gollum, Silent Slinker]] and [[Crude Bent Blade]] both rate higher here than in BR.
  * **Deck shape:** Menace and Equipment on Recruit tokens; hybrid [[Patient Instructor]] and [[Goblin Plate Mail]] glue the pair.

* **Other off-pairs:** RG, WG, etc. are **goodstuff** when those two colors are simply the best cards—no signpost plan. Do not force a theme; take the best curve and interaction from shared mechanics (Equipment, Ferocious overlap on [[Duskwatch Hunter]], etc.).

**Overlap:** Equipment bridges RW → WB/UB/Amass ([[Crude Bent Blade]], [[Goblin Plate Mail]]). Draw-twice package bridges WU → UB → UR. Recruit spills into WB. Base-black: red adds spells-into-Army; green adds ferocious starts. [[Thranduil, Sindarin Liege]] and [[Thranduil's Company]] fit any heavy-blue deck with land drops—[[Thranduil's Company]] can justify splashing green into WU.

### **Deck Shapes**

* **Build order:** (1) best castable cards for this 40, (2) interaction (~5–6), (3) lane density and curve. A 6-drop is fine when early plays are covered.
* **Lane targets:** BR spells-into-Army (~10–12 creatures, ~1 tapland); BG aggressive Wolves/Bears with ~8 early enablers; RW Equipment with high creature count and one-drop curve; WU/UB/UR draw-twice with ~6–7 two-drops and ~5–6 removal; GU green-heavy landfall stompy with answers—add [[Woodland Weavemaster]] only at ~6+ strong Elves.
* **Off-color decision:** If blue has 2+ draw-twice payoffs and 4+ enablers, compare WU vs UB vs UR from the **actual pool**—white Recruit count, black removal quality, or red two-drops/burn should break the tie.
* **Curve:** BG and BR want the most 1s and 2s. Blue shells often lack two-drops—[[Patient Instructor]], [[Bilbo Baggins, Burglar]], [[Óin the Brave]], [[Pinecone Strike]], and hybrid bodies fill gaps. Avoid padding with medium 4s/5s just to hit creature count. [[Magnificent End]] is a defensive two-drop—prioritize it in WU/UB, not RW/BR.
* **Interaction by color:** White [[Stone by Sunlight]] / [[Celebrate the Mountain-king]] / [[Magnificent End]]. Black [[Crude Bent Blade]] / [[Bilbo's Deadly Slice]] / [[Stir Up Trouble]] / [[Gnashing of Teeth]] / [[Reverent Howl]]. Green [[Quarrel]] / [[Warg Tactics]]. Red [[Pinecone Strike]] / [[Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern]]. Blue [[Uneasy Partings]] / [[Enchanted River's Grasp]] / [[Sound the Trumpets]] (with instant-heavy deck)—prefer other colors for removal when available.
* **Mana:** On-color duals first. Aggressive lanes: **one** dual or [[Hobbit Hole]] max. WU/UB/GU: 2–3 [[Lake-town]] / [[Hobbit Hole]] when splashes or landfall payoffs justify tap lands. [[The Lonely Mountain]] in BR/RW. Splash basics when the payoff is in the 40.

### **Common Mistakes**

* Picking a lane from tier lists instead of the pool's best mix of card quality, synergy, and mana.
* Running **2–3 taplands in BR/BG/RW** while also playing one-drops—either skip the one-drop or cap fixing at one tap source.
* **Splashing white bombs into BR** instead of building WB when white and black are both deep.
* **Splashing blue into RW**—blue high-rarity cards do not carry Equipment/Dwarf shells.
* Building **GU without landfall payoffs** ([[Mirkwood Pathmaker]], [[Silvan Reveler]], [[Wood Elves]]) or forcing **[[Woodland Weavemaster]]** without ~6+ strong Elves.
* Playing **[[Wood Elves]] in BG** instead of Rabbit/Pursuer → [[Desolation Prowler]] sequencing.
* Treating **[[Mirkwood Nurturer]] as landfall support**—it is off-curve bounce; fine for value loops in UB/WU, not a turn-six GU plan.
* **Splashing [[The Chief Warg]] or [[Chief Warg's Company]]** into BR—on-curve in BG only.
* Auto-playing gold **signposts** ([[Bolg of the North]], [[Thorin Oakenshield]], [[Silvan Reveler]], [[The Chief Warg]]) over cheaper on-lane cards—only [[Bard the Bowman]] consistently earns its slot; [[Thorin Oakenshield]] is an equip carrier, [[Silvan Reveler]] needs landfall density.
* Slotting **[[Gandalf, Wandering Wizard]]** in blue decks; passing **[[Gandalf, Spark Starter]]** in BR when curve is low.
* **[[Woodland Weavemaster]] in BG** or in GU without Elf density—it belongs in landfall-heavy GU with ~6+ strong Elves, or nowhere.
* Overpaying for **[[Dáin, Lord of the Iron Hills]]** without ~6–8 Dwarves already in the 40.
* **Expensive Equipment** ([[Sting, Bilbo's Sword]], [[Orcrist, Goblin-cleaver]]) over [[Dwarven Mattock]] / [[Crude Bent Blade]] / [[Goblin Plate Mail]].
* **[[Desert Were-Worm]]** and other high-curve traps with no early board.
* Slow **control-blue** plans without Recruit bodies or lifelink to close after stabilizing.
* **BR sacrifice package** ([[Snowslope Hunter]], [[Stir Up Trouble]]) without extra fodder.
* Filling GU with **weak blue Elves** just to raise Elf count—play good Elves only.
* Skimping on **lifelink** in WU after stabilizing on low life ([[Bard the Bowman]], [[Master's Councillors]]).
