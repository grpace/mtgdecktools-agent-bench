### **Supplemental Context: "Edge of Eternities" Limited Format**

This deck is being built for the **"Edge of Eternities" limited format**. The following information outlines the specific mechanics and archetypes for this set, which are critical for constructing a competitive deck. Please use this context in conjunction with the general deck-building instructions.

***

### **Key Set Mechanics**

1.  **Spacecrafts & Stations**: Spacecrafts are artifacts that become creatures after accumulating charge counters from the "Station" ability. A successful deck needs a good mix of both Spacecrafts and Station enablers.
2.  **Warp**: An alternative, cheaper casting cost that exiles the spell at the next end step. This provides a significant tempo advantage and enables "Void."
3.  **Void**: Cards with "Void" gain bonuses when you have a certain number of cards in exile. Warp is a primary enabler for this mechanic.
4.  **Chronomancy**: Allows you to scry a certain number of cards from your library, then draw a card. A powerful card selection mechanic.
5.  **Living Metal**: A keyword ability on some artifacts that allows them to become creatures until the end of the turn.

### **Archetype Guide**

*   **Azorius (White-Blue): Chronomancy Control**: Focuses on controlling the board with removal and counterspells, using Chronomancy for card advantage to find key finishers like large Spacecrafts.
*   **Dimir (Blue-Black): Warp/Void Tempo**: Utilizes cheap Warp spells to build a quick board presence and enable powerful Void payoffs. Highly aggressive and tempo-oriented.
*   **Rakdos (Black-Red): Artifact Aggro**: An aggressive strategy centered around "Living Metal" artifacts and other low-cost artifact creatures, aiming to overwhelm the opponent quickly.
*   **Gruul (Red-Green): Station/Spacecraft Ramp**: A ramp strategy focused on accelerating into powerful Stations to quickly charge up and crew massive Spacecrafts.
*   **Selesnya (Green-White): Go-Wide Tokens**: A strategy that builds a wide board of creature tokens, often enhanced by anthem effects or cards that benefit from having many creatures.
*   **Orzhov (White-Black): Aristocrats**: A sacrifice-themed archetype that benefits from creatures dying, using tokens or cheap creatures as fodder for powerful effects.
*   **Izzet (Blue-Red): Spellslinger**: Focuses on casting a high volume of instant and sorcery spells to trigger abilities on creatures and generate value.
*   **Golgari (Black-Green): Graveyard Recursion**: A midrange strategy that uses the graveyard as a resource, recurring creatures and gaining value from self-mill and reanimation.
*   **Boros (Red-White): Equipment Aggro**: An aggressive strategy focused on equipping creatures with powerful equipment to deal significant combat damage.
*   **Simic (Green-Blue): +1/+1 Counters**: A midrange strategy centered around placing +1/+1 counters on creatures and benefiting from synergies with those counters.
