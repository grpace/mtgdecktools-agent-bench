### **Reality Fracture (FRA) Limited Context**
**FRA supplement only.** The main deck-building prompt already defines your card pool and pool-only rules. Use **your own card evaluation for raw power** and this section for **lane fit, density, curve, mana, and how cards work together in the 40**. Card names in [[]] are examples of what to look for in your pool—not cards to add. **Build the best 40 from the actual pool.** Pool is FRA Play Boosters plus SPG 159–168 bonus hits when present; FRC commander cards are not in boosters—ignore them. Released 2026-10-02.

**Overrides generic Limited defaults:** Tempo-first format—curve out 2–4, hold cheap interaction, finish with evasion/trample/Jace flow. Match curve to the pool's plan: low-curve pressure runs leaner on lands/tapped duals; selection/ramp plans go slightly bigger. Default **15–17 creatures, 5–6 interaction, 17 lands**; pressure builds stay at 15+ bodies—pings and tricks are reach on top of creatures, not replacements. Cap tapped duals at **1–2** in one-drop pressure builds; slower builds run more. **[[Unsummon]] (1-mana bounce)** is a premium blue card—counts as interaction (tempo, protection, ETB rebuy, counter reset, prowess trigger).

**Compare from the pool, not the published order.** Start from the deepest color you actually have. White-deep: compare RW vs WU vs GW vs WB. Blue-deep: compare WU vs UB vs UR vs GU. Black-deep: compare BR vs BG vs UB vs WB. Red-deep: compare BR vs UR vs RW vs RG. Green-deep: compare RG vs GW vs BG vs GU. For each candidate, count from this pool: castable gold, enablers vs payoffs (using the Math lines below), curve through turns 1–4, interaction count, and mana. Pick the 40 with the best mix—not the lane of your best single card, and never a lane whose engine pieces are missing from the pool. Weigh strategy supply over color depth—a shared color is survivable, but poached enablers break the 40.

**How to choose (no lane is pre-ranked):** a lane earns the slot only when this pool supplies its pieces—surveil triggers plus flyers for WU; mill/surveil plus a threshold or reanimation payoff for UB; cheap attackers plus repeatable pings for BR; Heartwood makers plus a trampling finish for RG; repeatable lifegain plus counter payoffs for GW; recursion plus an outlet for WB; cheap noncreatures plus prowess bodies for UR; top end plus removal for BG; removal plus a bomb or rebuy engine (or a real 1–4 curve plus counter rewards) for RW; empowers plus a loyalty payoff plus interaction for GU. Rate plus curve plus mana beats a forced signpost every time.

### **Format Basics**

* **Ten lanes.** Five allied schools (WU Fatehold, UB Theorix, BR Stingerquill, RG Konstrari, GW Vigorbloom) plus five enemy pairs (WB Attrition, UR Prowess, BG Bestiary, RW Army, GU Mastery). Each school has an **uncommon Charm** plus gold uncommons.
* **Echoed pairs.** Every booster has a complete echoed pair (same character/type, **different colors**, same rarity, uncommon+) plus one extra legend. Pairs are designed to **not share a deck**—play the half your mana supports.
* **Legend density.** Most uncommons are legendary. Multiples are legal; the legend rule puts the extra into your graveyard (it isn't a sacrifice: sacrifice payoffs don't trigger), so keep the copy with counters or better position and prefer immediate board impact over setup pieces.
* **Empower everywhere.** Appears in all five colors at high as-fan, including [[Academic Ascent]] (pump plus empower), [[Campus Crier]] (2-mana 3/1, yards itself to empower), [[Protege's Awakening]] (empower 6 plus draw), [[No Admittance]] (3 damage plus empower), [[Arcane Amphisbaena]] (1/1 deathtouch plus empower 2). You will make a Jace token without trying—plan to feed it, protect it, and spend loyalty deliberately.
* **Duals need a planeswalker.** Ten common duals (five Annexes plus five enemy Commons) enter tapped **unless you control a planeswalker**—Jace token counts. Early empower turns them on; without one they are tapped duals. Fixing beyond duals: landcyclers ([[Hexhaven Battalion]], [[Undulating Witness]], [[Apex Witchstalker]], [[Awaken the Inferno]], [[Vinelasher Adept]]), [[Room of Refuge]], [[Murmuring Volume]] (multicolor/ramp glue—fixing plus acceleration plus filtering), [[Fblthp, Knows the Way]] (tutors differently-named basics plus body—premium fixing for splashes), [[Loot, the Nexus]] (adds by power variety).
* **Removal answers Jace.** [[Extended Absence]] (exile creature/planeswalker), [[Silence the Echo]] (sac fodder or pay extra to kill), [[No Admittance]] / [[Wrath of the Bloodmane]] (burn), [[Clash of Elements]] (bounce to top/bottom), [[Countersculpt]] / [[Icy Reception]] (counters), [[Compel Brutality]] (bite with power or loyalty) all hit tokens—never leave banked loyalty up.
* **Prepare is school glue.** Each school shares one prepared spell at common/uncommon; rares/mythics are unique. Upkeep re-prepare engines ([[Paradox Shaper]] (mill engine), [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]] (ping engine), [[Woodwork Prodigy]] (Heartwood engine)) are card advantage once protected—unprotected they rate below average, so only count them as engines with protection or redundancy already in the pool. One-shot bodies need **both halves** playable. Prepared cards are **creatures everywhere**—count them as creatures, not spells.
* **Two-color default.** Splash only a 3+ mana bomb or premium answer with real fixing (duals online plus cyclers)—sealed fixing stretches to a light third color for raw power when covered, and an extra tapped dual is worth the 6th–7th answer. Aggro lanes stay two colors for curve consistency; slow lanes (ramp, yard, control) splash freely with counted fixing. Double-pip splashes only for bombs or premium answers: 6–7 mana cards need ~6 sources (duals plus basics plus cyclers—late cards have time to find them, e.g. [[Kindred Judgment]] (7-mana wipe of all but one creature type)); cheaper double-pip cards need 7–8. Never splash 1–2 drops or the echo half.

### **Key Mechanics** (deckbuilding lens)

1. **Empower Jace N:** No Jace token → create a blue Jace token then put N loyalty on it. Have one → add N. Token: **-1: Surveil 1**, **-3: Draw a card**, not legendary. Most empowers are 1–2: stack two casts to draw (spending rules under Jace math). Cash big empowers the same turn ([[Way of the Wildspeaker]] (empower 7) into Beast/Leviathan with [[Kiora of Salt and Sand]] (loyalty-attack payoff))—bounce/exile punishes banking. Value payoffs: [[Jace's Machinations]] (empower 8 plus instant-speed loyalty), [[Repurposed Enforcer]] (empower on attack by creature count), [[Mindseeker Oculus]] (3-mana 2/1, empower 4 = near-instant draw), ten **Way** enchantments (ETB empower plus team loyalty ability, e.g. [[Way of the Healer]] (makes Cadets), [[Way of the Pyromancer]] (adds red)).
2. **Prepare / Prepared:** Creature carries a spell copy in exile. Cast it (pay costs) → creature becomes unprepared. Cannot re-prepare while prepared—cast first. Leaving or stealing gives the spell to the new controller. [[Unsummon]] rebuys ETB prepare bodies and resets one-shot users with counters.
3. **School spells:** **WU [[Peer Review]]** (make 2/2 Cadet, surveil 1) on [[Prudent Fateseer]] (team pump per surveil), [[Semester Foreseer]], [[Fatehold Chronologist]] (2-mana 1/2 flyer plus Cadet). **UB [[Omit Variables]]** (mill three) on [[Paradox Shaper]], [[Theorix Metamage]], [[Void Extrapolator]]. **BR [[Vicious Verse]]** (1-mana ping) on [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]], [[Hallway Heckler]], [[Whiplash Wordsmith]]. **RG [[Soul Tether]]** (make Heartwood) on [[Woodwork Prodigy]], [[Heartwood Crafter]], [[Konstrari Improviser]] (2-mana body that curves into turn-3 Heartwood). **GW [[Seed Suture]]** (+1/+1 plus 1 life) on [[Vigorbloom Vanguard]] (vigilance lord), [[Emergency Phytomedic]], [[Blossom-Blessed Angel]].
4. **Heartwood:** Red-green artifact, **{T}: Add {R} or {G}**. Enters untapped—spend it the turn it arrives (e.g. T3 [[Woodwork Prodigy]], T4 [[Soul Tether]] plus a 2-drop). Extra makers: [[Hungering Puppetbeast]], [[Aerid Konstrari]] (mythic: Heartwoods on entry and death). Finishers: [[Craftwork Crusher]] (7-mana modal trampler), [[Craterclaw Colossus]] (mass trample by artifacts).
5. **Cadet:** Colorless 2/2 Wizard Soldier. From [[Peer Review]], [[Recursive Recruitment]] (two Cadets, flashback, counters by yard size), [[Stingerquill Charm]] (hasty), Way abilities. Carries counters, pressures walkers, feeds sac counts—but is a bad sac target (2/2s do not want to die).
6. **Threshold (7+ in yard):** [[Proft, Sinister Mastermind]] (uncastable until online; discard-mode -3/-1 backup), [[Null Summoner]] (exiles from hand, casts only at threshold—slow), [[Theorix Metamage]] (3-mana 2/3, gains flying at threshold—serviceable filler that grinds to 3/3 flyers on schedule in counted pools) / [[Void Extrapolator]] (2-mana 2/2, becomes 3/3 at threshold), [[Loot, the Anomaly]] (-2/4, sac outlet locked until threshold—outlet, not engine). Rule: ~6+ mill/surveil enablers before any threshold card earns a slot. Prefer cheap spells that fill the yard while affecting board over dedicated mill that wastes mana. Yard stockers: [[Omit Variables]] bodies, [[Theorix Charm]] (mill then draw), [[Something Worth Saving]] (mill four plus recur), [[Liliana the Repentant]] (mill per entry, exhaust reanimate).
7. **Charms:** Flexible instants—pick the mode the turn needs. [[Fatehold Charm]] (draw-empower, bounce, or +1/+2), [[Theorix Charm]] (mill-draw, kill, or counter noncreature unless pay 2), [[Stingerquill Charm]] (ping, hasty Cadet, or first strike plus deathtouch), [[Konstrari Charm]] (counters plus trample, removal, or Heartwood), [[Vigorbloom Charm]] (hexproof save or counter-plus-fight).
8. **[[Unsummon]] role:** Premium blue card. Offensive tempo (clear blocker), defensive save (fizzle removal/tricks), value rebuy (own ETBs), counter hate (reset them), prowess/noncreature trigger, answer to reanimated fatties and [[Bestial Incursion]] (4/4 trampler plus flashback) tokens. Count it as interaction in any blue deck; multiples scale with ETB density and prowess counts.

### **Draft Archetypes**

Each lane lists **signposts** first. For every candidate lane, count enablers vs payoffs from this pool using its Math line; cut the payoff without density.

* **WU Fatehold — surveil tempo — [[Prudent Fateseer]], [[Desperate Futurescribe]]**
  * **Plan:** Flyers plus Cadets triggering team pumps every turn. [[Prudent Fateseer]] (prepared, +1/+0 team per surveil), [[Desperate Futurescribe]] (combat pump becomes a counter after surveil), [[Diviner of Victory]] (grows per trigger), [[Denzilore Fatehold]] (mythic flash flyer: counter on each creature per trigger).
  * **Core:** [[Proft, Consulting Detective]] (pay 2 per trigger: counter plus card), [[Saheeli, Consul of Oversight]] (Thopter per trigger—have Jace out the turn it lands), [[Fatehold Chronologist]] (premium multi-body), [[Campus Crier]] (3/1 early trade into yard-empower 2 at instant speed—let a low Jace die, empower end of turn, don't chump-shield), [[Tomik, Orzhov Lawmage]] (2/1 flyer, caps walker attackers at one—Jace guard), [[Surveillance Phantasm]] (self-sufficient surveil attacker plus Jace guard—still better with counted triggers), [[Predictive Preparations]] (1-mana counters plus flashback—free when surveilled away; first cut without a counter-dense or surveil-mill plan), [[Unsummon]] (protect triggers, rebuy ETBs), [[Prophesied End]] (1-mana flexible kill—clean vs attackers, concedes a card vs blockers; best where flyers force attacks).
  * **Math:** Surveil trigger nearly every turn earns the payoffs; Jace -1 covers blank turns. Deep at two—curve out, do not rush -3.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on repeated triggers plus flyers from this pool. One payoff with no enablers does not carry the lane.

* **UB Theorix — yard control — [[Paradox Shaper]], [[Recursive Recruitment]]**
  * **Plan:** Cheap interaction plus card flow, then threshold/flashback top end. [[Paradox Shaper]] (upkeep re-mill plus bottom-a-card control), [[Recursive Recruitment]] (early bodies, late giant flashback—top yard priority; splashes with counted blue/black access and is a top rebuy target), [[Dark Matter Manipulator]] (mill three plus yard-scaled body).
  * **Core:** [[Last Gasp]] (-3/-3), [[Extended Absence]] (exile), [[Unsummon]] (tempo plus rebuy), [[Twinned Vision]] (draw plus flashback—also a mill target), [[Theoretical Necromancer]] (3-mana 4/1—fine to cast on turn 3 as a roadblock that trades with 4/4 Beasts; later exiles from yard to rebuy a bomb or key creature), [[Rewrite Regrets]] (reanimate MV ≤6 plus empower 2), [[Uldaros Theorix]] (mythic yard-type free-cast), [[Null Summoner]] (triggers on cast only—reanimation misses) / [[Proft, Sinister Mastermind]] (threshold finishers), [[Sanctum Lurker]] (Jace ping-gain engine—needs a Jace deck).
  * **Fblthp mill-out build:** [[Fblthp, Impossibly Lost]] (2-mana; draw two when you deal combat damage on your turn, win if your library is empty, shuffles himself back) is the one true build-around—you always see him again. Shape: control-ish removal plus card draw plus fast self-mill ([[Paradox Shaper]] most important, [[Theorix Metamage]], [[Void Extrapolator]], [[Something Worth Saving]] (best version runs green)), 2–3 [[Theoretical Necromancer]] to rebuy a milled Fblthp, evasive connectors for the final hit ([[Theorix Metamage]] flying at threshold, [[Undulating Witness]] (5-mana 3/5 flyer plus landcycling), [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive]]), and yard-useful cards ([[Twinned Vision]], [[Blessed Ghoul]]). [[Unsummon]] saves Fblthp from exile. Do not rely on looping your library forever with [[Paradox Shaper]] alone—one removal spell on it loses the game.
  * **Math:** Mill alone does nothing—count cheap defense too ([[Mabel, Bitter Recluse]] (1-mana deathtouch plus walker-counter removal—hold past turn one unless curve is set))
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on removal plus a payoff or reanimation from this pool. Mill spells with nowhere to go do not earn slots; [[Rank Rat]] (1/1 discard) earns its slot with a payoff or outlet ([[Way of the Deathbringer]] curve, bounce-rebuy loops, discard web)—without one it feeds opposing threshold for free.

* **BR Stingerquill — ping aggro — [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]], [[Grim Repriser]]**
  * **Plan:** Cheap attackers plus repeatable pings that enable each other. [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]] (upkeep re-ping), [[Grim Repriser]] (prowess plus yard return after noncombat damage), [[Master of Barbs]] (team pump per noncombat damage), [[Massacre Girl, Most Wanted]] (ping/drain per death), [[Ingris Stingerquill]] (mythic: attackers ping).
  * **Core:** [[Hallway Heckler]] / [[Whiplash Wordsmith]] (prepared ping bodies), [[No Admittance]] (3 anywhere plus empower), [[Wrath of the Bloodmane]] (4 damage, cheaper with legend), [[Screeching Soulbreaker]] (1/4 flyer ping—playable here with multipliers), [[Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage]] (prowess plus +1 to all noncombat damage—doubles 1-pings), [[Koth, the Geomancer]] (landfall ping plus red—needs Mountains/red density; Heartwoods don't trigger landfall), [[Cast Away Doubt]] (draw-two plus ping—earns its slot with the ping web online), [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Pursuer]] (double strike—high variance, needs pump/trample pairing and an untap), [[Chandra's Emberling]] (weak rate—density only, wants evasion), [[Danitha, Spear of Agony]] (2/2 first strike—grows per spell targeting opp; wants [[Vicious Verse]] volume).
  * **Math:** Pressure from turn one and close with reach. Best package: [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]] (most important piece—repeatable ping plus spell trigger every turn) plus [[Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage]] (turns each ping into 2 and grows) plus [[Winter, Team Player]] (team +1/+0 per noncreature—payoff only in heavy-red ping builds, weak elsewhere). One ping enabler is not a deck—count attackers plus surviving ping bodies from this pool. A removed engine leaves dead cards, so rate plus removal beats the theme without both.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on cheap attackers plus repeatable pings. One burn spell with no engine does not carry the lane.

* **RG Konstrari — Heartwood ramp — [[Woodwork Prodigy]], [[Craftwork Crusher]]**
  * **Plan:** Bodies that ramp into the biggest mana and tramplers. [[Woodwork Prodigy]] (upkeep re-Heartwood), [[Konstrari Improviser]] (turn-2 body into turn-3 Heartwood—guarantees curve), [[Craftwork Crusher]] (modal finisher: removal, Cadet, or cards).
  * **Core:** [[Heartwood Crafter]] (tap-mana restriction is fake—fuels own [[Soul Tether]] and flashback; T1 Crafter, T2 Heartwood, T3 [[Bestial Incursion]], T4 flashback it), [[Tenured Tethermage]] (land for two Heartwoods), [[Greenhouse Propagator]] (body plus mana—best here), [[Tether Technician]] (4/5 reach, ETB may discard to shock 2—reach plus outlet), [[Yoshimaru, Scrappy Stray]] (1/1, ETB fight via another body plus 6-mana counter sink—removal that leaves a body), [[Bestial Incursion]] (4/4 trampler plus flashback), [[Kiora of Fire and Ashes]] (Dragon maker plus 8-mana Dragon stream—proven closer demanding answers), [[Pia, Determined Rebuilder]] / [[Inspired Tethermage]] (3-drops with 6-mana sinks—safe top-end bridges), [[Ghalta the Unstoppable]] (discounted mass trample—plays in most green decks; count ~5 four-power bodies so it costs 5), [[Craterclaw Colossus]] (artifact-scaled trample), [[Verdant Kraken]] (land tokens), [[Draconic Visitor]] (artifact tokens into 5/5 Dragons—needs untap), [[Garruk, Curse Breaker]] (mythic draw plus untaps), [[Marwyn, the Preserver]] (lands hexproof plus yard-land return—long-game mana; loops [[Room of Refuge]] (sac for two counters) as a late-game sink, so take Room over an extra dual when Marwyn is in), [[Ruric Thar, Magecrusher]] (big reach beater—a clear step below the guaranteed-value finishers). [[Craftwork Crusher]] (RRGG modal trampler) ramps out T5–6 in RG—triple-pipped cost stays in-lane despite Heartwoods.
  * **Math:** ~6+ Heartwood/artifact sources before count payoffs. Skip vanilla 3s; sink 3s ([[Pia, Determined Rebuilder]], [[Inspired Tethermage]]) are bridges, then load 5s/6s—turn-3 Heartwood means turn-4 five-drop or double-spell.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on makers plus trampling finish from this pool. Ramp with no finish does not carry the lane; [[Wrecking Gecko]] (overcosted beater) is a weak fit here—count it only if the pool needs that role. [[Living Library]] (0/4 tuck-answers body) is a serviceable 23rd with counted need for early walker cover plus tuck answers—never spend a pick on it.

* **GW Vigorbloom — counters/lifegain — [[Vigorbloom Vanguard]], [[Bloombrute]]**
  * **Plan:** Counter shell with lifegain holding off aggro. [[Vigorbloom Vanguard]] (vigilance lord), [[Bloombrute]] (lifegain into draw once per turn plus trample-lifelink mode that self-triggers).
  * **Core:** [[Titanbones, Towering Heart]] (payoff only—two counters per lifegain; 4/3 dies to [[Last Gasp]]/[[No Admittance]]; needs a repeatable engine, then rates as a 22nd/23rd card—never spend density to include it), [[Unflinching Hortimancer]] (2/1 ward 1, counter per lifegain—earns its slot with a repeatable engine; without one, [[Campus Crier]] (3/1 into yard-empower) rates better), [[Graft Surgeon]] (enters with counter, moves on death), [[Predictive Preparations]] / [[Generous Revival]] (counter plus flashback/reanimate—first cut without a counter-dense plan), [[Fateshaper Aspirant]] (legend recur or counter), [[Rescue Girl, First Responder]] (3-mana 1/3 flyer, rebuys a permanent each turn—only with 2+ removal ETBs, see RW), [[Enlightened Confidant]] (mythic lifegain into surveil-draw), [[Ajani Resolute]] (mythic loyalty per lifegain), [[Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion]] (extra counter per placement), [[Kwia Vigorbloom]] (mythic lifelink Lotus maker).
  * **Math:** Pick one axis—counters white-x or lifegain. Half of each is a trap. Counters comes together more often ([[Yoshimaru, Beloved Companion]] plus counter bodies); lifegain needs specific engines. Repeatable engines ([[Koth of the Homestead]] (landfall life plus Plains counters—wants heavy Plains for 2–3 triggers), [[Greenhouse Propagator]], [[Solarium Sentry]], [[Medic's Kitesail]] (2-mana equipment: +1/+0, flying, gain 1 per attack—evasion plus a trigger every turn)) beat one-shot [[Seed Suture]]. Lifegain loop: [[Way of the Paradox]] (life per loyalty activation) plus [[Ajani Resolute]] (loyalty per lifegain) plus [[Inspired Tethermage]] (grows per loyalty)—any deck that wants [[Unflinching Hortimancer]] wants [[Ajani Resolute]]. Almost no keyword lifelink—do not wait for it.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on redundant engines plus counter payoffs from this pool. One lifegain card plus one counter card with no redundancy does not carry the lane.

* **WB Attrition — recursion/attrition — [[Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord]]**
  * **Plan:** White midrange bodies plus black removal; attack with menace and counters. [[Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord]] (2-mana 2/3, drains per death, sacs into menace threat) is rate plus upside. [[Twisted Fates]] (kill nonland plus team counters) rewards wide attacking boards.
  * **Core:** [[Hexhaven Battalion]] (3 Cadets plus empower plus cycle), [[Fatehold Chronologist]], [[Campus Crier]], [[Generous Revival]] (reanimate ≤3 plus counter plus flashback), [[Gallia, Tragic Host]] (2/1 menace plus repeatable yard return, no finality), [[Blessed Ghoul]] (lifelink yard return), [[Winter, Tormented Loner]] (edict outlet—sacs post-value Jace or itself; needs real fodder), [[Thalia, the Survivor]] (3/4 lifelink plus noncreature tax—holds stalls, takes counters), [[Break Under Pressure]] / [[Extended Absence]] / [[Last Gasp]] (removal suite). [[Way of the Deathbringer]] plus multiples of Edgar is the sac-density signal—both need expendable bodies first.
  * **Math:** No sack-draw engine exists, and 2/2 Cadets are bad fodder—count recursion plus outlets from this pool; do not run weak bodies to feed outlets.
  * **Enter/avoid:** White depth plus black removal plus recursion or an outlet. A premium gold uncommon ([[Twisted Fates]] (kill plus team counters)) justifies evaluating the pair on rate even without the engine. Without those, it still evaluates as white-based good-cards—compare on rate, curve, and answers. [[Rank Rat]] (1/1 discard) earns its slot with a payoff or outlet—without one it feeds opposing threshold for free.

* **UR Prowess — spells tempo — [[Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar]]**
  * **Plan:** Control is the default (blue draw plus red removal) and needs 3–4 resilient closers—draw alone is not inevitability. Prowess tempo (one creature plus a stream of cheap spells) is the other build. [[Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar]] (Thopter per noncreature, Thopters haste—must untap to pay; earns its slot only with counted untap cover) is support, never the plan. [[Clash of Elements]] (flexible bounce-burn that also answers big Jace). [[Woodwork Prodigy]] (re-prepares [[Soul Tether]] each upkeep) feeds Saheeli a Thopter every turn—a home for her in ramp-leaning red builds. Control frequently drifts Grixis off black removal or payoffs in the pool.
  * **Core:** Stay low: [[Stingerquill Voxmancer]] (cross-lane ping engine), [[Cryotheory Adept]] (conditional filler—prowess plus overpriced yard-stun; needs 8+ cheap noncreatures), [[Tomik, Izzet Sparkmage]], [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Pursuer]] (double strike prowess—needs pairing to connect), [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive]] (1/3, 1-tough unblockable plus tough Jace guard), [[Chandra's Emberling]] (weak rate—density only), [[Pompous Battlemage]] / [[Pyre Rhymer]] / [[Variable Chaser]] (prepared prowess bodies), [[Twinned Vision]] (double trigger plus anti-flood—happy with 2–3 copies), [[Tam's Resistance]] (hybrid sorcery: +1/+1 plus vigilance plus empower 4—prowess burst, pairs with flyers), [[Unsummon]] (tempo plus trigger), [[Plan for All Outcomes]] (top/bottom plus empower per turn—no trigger the turn it enters, so deploy early; earns its slot in any spell-dense list, triggers on opp turns with instants), [[Traxos, Academy Guardian]] (1/5 flyer vigilance, discounts to 2 with a spell—plan a turn-3/4 double-spell, not a lone turn-4 tap-out; pairs with [[Unsummon]] plus [[Blazing Crescendo]] for combo-kill turns with [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Pursuer]]), [[Geist of Saint Thalia]] (noncreature discount—saves real mana on Ways and removal), [[Artifist Acumen]] (mass first strike plus card—UR-only burst, dead in curve decks).
  * **Math:** Trigger density beats top end. Prowess builds play 10–12 creatures; [[Cryotheory Adept]] must attack as a 3 and [[Chandra's Emberling]] must scale most turns—playable only here. Cantrips plus prepared spells count twice; 6-drop prowess cards are finishers, not the plan. [[Ruric Thar, Biomagus]] (double prowess) evaluates better as a control finisher.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on 2+ prowess bodies plus 8+ cheap noncreatures from this pool. Spells with nothing to trigger do not carry the lane.

* **BG Bestiary — stompy plus removal — [[Hapatra, the Desert Fang]], [[Primal Witchstalker]]**
  * **Plan:** Black removal plus green top end smashing through stalls. [[Primal Witchstalker]] (2/1 menace, mill four plus land back—ramp plus yard), [[Hapatra, the Desert Fang]] (3/3, -1/-1 scaled to yard size—needs a big card in yard; landcyclers stock it), [[Vraska, the Cutting Glare]] (rare 3-mana 4/4 deathtouch, destroys a permanent on entry at 6+ lands—count lands and cyclers).
  * **Core (good-stuff):** [[Arcane Amphisbaena]] (1/1 deathtouch plus empower—protects Jace), [[Ferocity of the Hunt]] (flash deathtouch that returns—best on small ETB-value bodies so the return replays the ETB, e.g. [[Mindseeker Oculus]] in Sultai), [[Rampart Hunter]] (3/3 grants deathtouch), [[Mabel, Bitter Recluse]] (1/1 deathtouch plus counter kill), [[Something Worth Saving]] (mill plus recur plus life—underrated filler that fills while digging), [[Carnivorous Cultivator]] (prepared deathtouch plus land recur), [[Bestial Incursion]] (4/4 trampler—stall-breaker on rate), [[Garruk, Curse Breaker]] (mythic draw engine). [[Hunter's Axe]] (1-mana trample-or-deathtouch per attack) is the only same-attacker pairing in-lane—upside when present, not the default (other trample/deathtouch pairings need off-lane charms).
  * **Core (reanimator alt):** [[Rewrite Regrets]] (reanimate plus empower—deck centerpiece), [[Restore with Empathy]] (return plus life—stand-in when Regrets is missing), [[Liliana the Repentant]] (mill plus exhaust reanimate), big cyclers as targets, [[Bestial Incursion]] (flashback payoff). Folds to [[Unsummon]]—prefer stompy when unsure.
  * **Math:** Green top end plus black removal first; yard combo only with reanimation plus targets counted from this pool.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on bodies plus answers. Yard cards with no payoffs do not earn slots. [[Hapatra, the Desert Fang]] plus [[Primal Witchstalker]] splash off a Forest plus cyclers—green works as support, not only as a ramp main.

* **RW Army — counters midrange-grind — [[Mabel, Valley Hero]], [[Warrior's Blades]]**
  * **Plan (default):** Removal plus value counters bodies, closed by a bomb. Cheap removal ([[No Admittance]]) fills the 2-slot, a bomb finishes ([[Ajani Unrelenting]] (6-mana walker: Cadet per activation, -3 deals 4 to each creature except your tokens), [[Kiora of Fire and Ashes]]), [[Fateshaper Aspirant]] (5-mana 3/4, returns a legendary card) rebuys it, and [[Rescue Girl, First Responder]] loops removal ETBs while [[Teyo, Lightshield Expert]] protects her (flash Teyo in response to removal, pick Teyo up next turn—opponents need two answers in one turn). [[Warrior's Blades]] (3 damage plus 3 life on entry, +2/+1, equips cheaper per counter) is removal plus a threat in either build.
  * **Aggro build (only with a real curve):** Counters on everything that enters—[[Mabel, Valley Hero]] (counters each entrant), first-strike two-drops, tricks. The aggro pieces are mid-rate; without a deep 1–4 curve they cannot close past incidental lifegain.
  * **Core:** [[Gallia, the Merrymaker]] (counter-haste lord—ground 2/1 poorly positioned vs flyers; needs the curve counted), [[Skilled Battlecarver]] (first-strike two-drop—huge with a counter), [[Charge the Sanctum]] (mass +2/+0 or targeted pump plus first strike plus counter—trick that leaves value), [[Heartstring Puller]] (3/1 trample plus Cadet—filler without a counted curve; pressure plus tricks beat bodies that do not change stalls), [[Winter, Team Player]] (convoke team pump—needs creatures plus spells together), [[Guiding Hydra]] (X counters plus redistribute), [[Rescue Girl, First Responder]] (rebuy engine—earns its slot only with 2+ high-impact removal ETBs: [[Lich's Relic]] (1-mana equipment, pay 2 on entry to destroy a creature or walker), [[Ajani's Anguish]] (X burn on entry), [[Vraska, the Cutting Glare]], [[Hapatra, the Desert Fang]] (multiples work at uncommon); [[Warrior's Blades]] (3 damage) and [[Plan for All Outcomes]] (4 mana per loop, ETB threats come back) are weaker loops. Ways, [[Mindseeker Oculus]], and [[Fateshaper Aspirant]] are support, not reasons—without the removal she is a 3-mana 1/3 flyer), [[Samut, Hazoret's Champion]] (mass haste), [[Danitha, Sword of Hope]] (draw engine—needs 9–10 triggers, not 4–5; must-answer reward), [[Teyo, Lightshield Expert]] (flash hexproof plus counter), [[Face Yourself]] (token-copy top end).
  * **Math:** Grind needs 5+ removal plus a bomb or rebuy engine. Aggro needs a real 1–4 curve in both colors; combat tricks plus first strike decide mirrors, and Jace loyalty is low value relative to pressure.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on removal plus a finisher (grind) or curve plus counter rewards (aggro) from this pool. Scattered gold with no early plays and no bomb does not carry the lane. Jace-vs-face in aggro: weigh a 1–2 loyalty token against face damage that ends the game.

* **GU Mastery — empower combo-control — [[Kiora of Salt and Sand]], [[Mind Meanderer]]**
  * **Plan:** Survive, feed Jace, convert loyalty into Leviathan/Beast wins. [[Kiora of Salt and Sand]] (loyalty attacks become unblockable plus -8 Leviathan for all walkers), [[Mind Meanderer]] (4/4 flyer, fights on entry, vigilance with Jace—attacks while guarding), [[Avatar of Burgeoning Echoes]] (mythic landfall empower plus land-count ultimate).
  * **Core:** [[Compel Brutality]] (Jace-loyalty bite kills most boards), [[Countersculpt]] (2-mana counter with Jace plus empower), [[Icy Reception]] (counters creature/legendary unless pay 3—hits legendary Ways), [[Unsummon]] (protect engine plus rebuy [[Mindseeker Oculus]] for loyalty swings), [[Inspired Tethermage]] (grows per loyalty plus late empower sink—needs multiple loyalty sources and pairs best with uptick Ways; 3/2 guards poorly alone), [[Jace, Reality Sculptor]] (empower by Islands), [[Entrust the Spark]] (sac walker into tutored walker), reach/fliers ([[Vinelasher Adept]] (cycle plus reach), fliers) to stop opposing flyers killing your Jace, [[Arni, Humble Scribe]] (loot engine), [[Tam's Resistance]] (hybrid sorcery: +1/+1 plus vigilance plus empower 4—cheap loyalty burst).
  * **Math:** Multiple empowers plus a loyalty payoff plus interaction. With [[Kiora of Salt and Sand]], favor big empowers ([[Tam's Resistance]], [[Protege's Awakening]]); the unblockable untap matters even without the Leviathan. Combos: [[Jace's Machinations]] (empower 8 plus instant-speed loyalty—flash in the 8/8 on the opponent's turn), [[Repurposed Enforcer]] (attack trigger empowers by creature count—Kiora keeps it unblockable, so it keeps feeding Jace). Draw is abundant—survival is scarce. Floor without combo is playable midrange (bounce plus bite plus 2/4s plus [[Mindseeker Oculus]]), but do not mistake it for the combo—no Kiora/Avatar means no Leviathan plan.
  * **Enter/avoid:** Compare on Kiora/Avatar plus answers for the combo from this pool; the floor without them evaluates as midrange. One Jace maker with no payoff does not carry the lane; neither does a list with no flyer/reach cover.

**Overlap:** Empower/surveil bridges WU → UB → GU. Pings/spells bridge BR ↔ UR. Ramp bridges RG ↔ GU. Counters bridge GW ↔ RW. Yard bridges UB ↔ WB/BG. Cadets, Heartwoods, Charms, [[Unsummon]], and hybrid one-drops splash across neighbors. Any lane wins with the best mix of **standalone power**, synergy, and mana.

### **Deck Shapes**

* **Build order:** (1) best castable cards, (2) interaction (~5–6, or 6–7 for midrange/over-the-top builds: white exile/tap, blue bounce/counters, black kill/exile, red burn, green bite), (3) lane density and curve. One 6-drop is fine with early plays covered. Counters count as interaction: [[Countersculpt]] (2-mana counter plus empower) and [[Icy Reception]] (hits creatures, walkers, legendary Ways) earn slots.
* **Common pillars:** White: [[Memory Trap]] (exile nonland), [[Your Fate Ends Here]] (MV3+ creature/walker kill plus surveil), [[Surgical Precision]] (kill toughness 4+ or draw-plus-life), [[Hexhaven Battalion]] (3 Cadets plus empower plus cycle—WU/WB priority; not splashable). Blue: [[Unsummon]] (tempo core), [[Mindseeker Oculus]] (empower 4 into draw), [[Surveillance Phantasm]] (blocks air, attacks after surveil when triggers are counted), [[Icy Reception]] (counter or -5/-0). Black: [[Multiply by Zero]] (0/0 instant—answers counters, premium trick), [[Extended Absence]] (exile walker/creature), [[Last Gasp]] (-3/-3), [[Silence the Echo]] (one copy—needs fodder). Red: [[No Admittance]] (3 anywhere plus empower), [[Wrath of the Bloodmane]] (4 damage, cheaper with legend), [[Violent Echoes]] (6 damage plus empower on excess). Green: [[Compel Brutality]] (bite—pairs with deathtouch and big Jace), [[Arcane Amphisbaena]] (deathtouch guard plus empower), [[Bestial Incursion]] (4/4 trampler plus flashback—strong on rate but adds no synergy; a fine slot, never a reason to be green), [[Jiang Yanggu, Never Alone]] (two bodies that untap tokens incl. Heartwoods).
* **Card flow:** Run 3–4 card-advantage sources or lose the grind—[[Twinned Vision]] (draw plus flashback looter, plays even in ramp), [[Protege's Awakening]] (slow draw-3 plus empower 6), [[Sphinx's Approach]] (draw-2, needs ~10–11 blue sources). More empowers means fewer raw-draw spells, and vice versa.
* **Match the plan to the pool's power:** With bombs, grind—recursion ([[Fateshaper Aspirant]], [[Blessed Ghoul]] (1-mana lifelink, returns to hand)) is worth it to rebuy them. Without an A-level card, build tempo and pressure ([[Unsummon]], evasion such as [[Medic's Kitesail]]) and end the game early—long-game engines with only good cards lose to decks with better late games. A pile of 2-for-1s with no shared plan gets outgrown by cohesive Jace decks. Match support to the bomb's game size: [[Aerid Konstrari]] wants ramp and late-game cards, not small-game black cards.
* **Curve:** Board presence first—a real 2-drop beats turn-2 landcycling (cyclers are fixing/late plays, not T2 plays, or you lose the Jace war). BR and aggro RW most 1s–2s (BR deepest one-drops; WU deepest two-drops). UB/GU slightly higher with selection. RG ramp on 2–3 into 5+. Never pad with medium 4s/5s for creature count. One-filler rule: one stat-only card is tolerable, two-plus is not—splash a B-level card such as [[Way of the Healer]] over a second [[Marwyn, the Preserver]], [[Budding Insurgent]], or off-plan [[Heartstring Puller]]. Conditional two-drops ([[Void Extrapolator]] (needs threshold), [[Unflinching Hortimancer]] (needs repeatable lifegain)) are minus cards outside their shell—cut them before a cycler or removal spell. Prepared bodies count as creatures; [[Unsummon]] and tricks do not.
* **Toughness breakpoint:** 4 toughness matters—dodges [[Last Gasp]] (-3/-3) and [[No Admittance]] (3 damage). Favor 2/4s and 4/5s ([[Blossom-Blessed Angel]] (2/4 flying vigilance), [[Tether Technician]] (4/5 reach), [[Traxos, Academy Guardian]] (1/5 flyer)) that hold stalls and guard Jace. [[Ghalta the Immovable]] (9-mana, discounted by greatest toughness; high-toughness creatures deal toughness damage and walls can attack) is a WU 20th–23rd card when the deck already has [[Surveillance Phantasm]] and [[Tetsuko Umezawa, Fugitive]] (makes low-power walls unblockable)—never build around it or add [[Living Library]] for it.
* **Mana:** Duals first but audit speed—early Jace turns them on, otherwise tapped. Heartwoods count as RG fixing. One basic per splash. One splash card needs 3–4 sources (dual online plus cyclers)—never bare basics; each extra splash card needs proportionally more fixing. Landcyclers double as fixing plus late plays. [[Theorist's Sanctum]] (repeat-empower land) is a blue pull in grind plans with time to activate.
* **Slot math:** One-shot prepare needs both halves playable. Upkeep engines are card advantage—protect with [[Unsummon]]/counters and keep casting the copies. Threshold needs ~6+ enablers; Heartwood-count needs ~6+ sources—never run weak artifacts for numbers. A school earns the 40 with ~4–5+ bodies sharing its prepared spell plus ~2 payoffs—fewer activators means good-cards instead of the school. Prowess needs 8+ cheap noncreatures; lifegain needs 2+ repeatable engines.
* **Jace math:** First empower makes the token; later ones grow it. -1 early for triggers/yard/duals; -3 when the card beats the loyalty; big loyalty cashed the same turn. Avoid 1–2 incidental empowers with no payoff (lone empower is just surveil-once). Way floors are playable alone: [[Way of the Healer]] (empower 5 into Cadet-plus-surveil engine—premium white pull; guard Jace once online, default to repeat -2), [[Way of the Mentor]] (lifegain-to-loyalty—unplayable; lifegain decks want pressure), [[Way of the Pyromancer]] (2-mana, +1 adds red—plays only in decks with many 2s and 4s, cheap creatures to guard Jace, and medium empowers (1–4); the +1 uses the turn's Jace activation, so big empowers that want to down-tick clash with it; uptick payoffs ([[Inspired Tethermage]], [[Plan for All Outcomes]]) are upside), [[Way of the Wildspeaker]] (empower 7—5-mana 4/4 floor even if answered). Cap Ways at ~3—they stack badly (one activation per turn, removal blows out banked loyalty, too many non-board cards). Rate [[Way of the Healer]] (Cadet engine) plus [[Way of the Mind Sculptor]] (draw engine, best grind-breaker) first, [[Way of the Paradox]] (empower 5, then life plus an extra land drop per loyalty activation—needs card draw and other empowers to keep hitting land drops; also a repeatable GW lifegain engine) next; [[Way of the Deathbringer]] (sac-to-4/4) only with expendable bodies counted ([[Rank Rat]], [[Gallia, Tragic Host]] (recurs from yard))—a standalone black aggro card, not an empower payoff. [[Way of the Mentor]] plus [[Way of the Necromancer]] (death-to-loyalty) are unplayable—the sac shell is unsupported. [[Way of the Wildspeaker]] (5-mana 4/4 floor) is mid-pack; [[Way of the Warlord]] (face-only burn) last; [[Way of the Pyromancer]] without cheap guards and 2s/4s underperforms—same rule. [[Way of the Cryomancer]] (copy next instant/sorcery) earns its slot with 7–8 removal or token spells to copy—[[Violent Echoes]] (excess damage refills loyalty for the next copy), [[Hexhaven Battalion]] (six Cadets), [[Recursive Recruitment]] (a copy of the flashback cast does not get the counters). Copying draw spells is not worth the setup. Against pressure, skip activating the turn it lands and keep loyalty at 5 for next turn's copy. Leave a blocker or deploy after opp taps out—opponents will attack it. Do not auto-fire -1 every turn: sitting on 1 loyalty forces attacks and banks a later draw. Attack opposing Jaces at 3+ loyalty to deny the draw (go face at 2 unless the attack is free—but always hit a 1-loyalty Jace over 1 face damage when the opponent has [[Way of the Paradox]], which gains that life back); protect your own at 3+. When unsure, cash -3 for the card; spread -1 surveils only when stalled or digging.
* **Parity breakers:** Every 40 needs a way through stalls—flying ([[Saheeli, Consul of Oversight]] Thopters, [[Surveillance Phantasm]]), menace, trample ([[Craterclaw Colossus]], [[Hunter's Axe]] plus deathtouch), burn reach ([[Vicious Verse]] copies, [[No Admittance]]), or mana sinks (Ways, [[Kiora of Fire and Ashes]] Dragons). Test every 40 against the format's most common game-enders—[[Kiora of Fire and Ashes]] (Dragon stream), [[Recursive Recruitment]] (Cadet flashback), [[Craftwork Crusher]] (modal trampler).
* **Tricks to respect (and run when in pool):** +2/+2 white for 2, +3/+1 red ([[Blazing Crescendo]] plus exile-play), +2/+2 green ([[Tethermage's Advantage]] plus untap), [[Charge the Sanctum]], [[Ferocity of the Hunt]] (flash deathtouch—never trade green bigs into it; answer or bounce instead), charm combat modes, flash bodies ([[Teyo, Lightshield Expert]] (hexproof), [[Divining Duelist]] (tap/untap/loot), [[Yuriko, Hope from the Shadows]] (kill or surveil 2), [[Teyo, Diamondblade Mage]] (deathtouch—pairs with [[Compel Brutality]] bite), [[Edgar, Moonlit Sovereign]] (flash grow)), counters ([[Countersculpt]] (only with a counted Jace source—overcosted cancel without one), [[Theorix Charm]]), discard ([[Rank Rat]], [[Tinybones, Pocket Nuisance]], [[Solve for Disappointment]]—keep a spare land late vs black).

### **Common Mistakes**

* Forcing WB sac or GU empower without the specific multiples/payoffs—play good-cards plus removal instead.
* Splashing the echo half instead of playing the on-color half in its lane.
* Banking big loyalty instead of cashing the same turn.
* Rushing -3 in surveil decks instead of reusing -1.
* Dedicated mill with no board (UB/BG)—cheap spells fill the yard while interacting.
* One-shot lifegain with no engine (GW)—need repeatable sources.
* Sitting on Heartwood mana (RG)—spend it the turn it arrives.
* Running weak artifacts/counters cards just to raise counts.
* Playing [[Rank Rat]] with no payoff or outlet—it feeds opposing threshold for free.
* Leaving narrow exile answers in the pool box—e.g. [[Refute Destiny]] (exiles green/blue creature or planeswalker plus surveil)—board them in after game one instead of forgetting them.
* [[Flickering Hound]] (4-mana 2/2 that blinks a creature per creature cast) as a body—needs several strong ETB creatures counted and dies to removal after you tap out; [[Rescue Girl, First Responder]] is the more flexible rebuy engine.
* [[Command the Stage]] (3-mana 2/2 Cadet that returns only after noncombat damage)—needs three casts to beat rate; avoid even in ping decks.
* [[Eye of Jace]] (1-mana upkeep surveil, drains at threshold)—surveil is abundant from Jace and other cards; it affects no board, so it is a last-resort playable only.
* [[Pia, Aether Ascetic]] (3-mana 2/2, discard to tutor an enchantment) without several good targets ([[Plan for All Outcomes]] best, [[Memory Trap]], [[Way of the Healer]]) and flashback cards to discard ([[Bestial Incursion]], [[Recursive Recruitment]])—discarding real cards is a cost in a land-hungry format.
* [[Yuriko, Blade of the Mighty]] (symmetrical combat lock shuts off your own tricks—do not run as a body).
* Spending slots on build-arounds without their pieces: [[Sphinx's Approach]] (needs 5 copies plus a Sphinx) and oversized vanillas ([[Yargle, Glutton of Urborg]] unplayable); [[Precise Redaction]] (white/black-only) is sideboard; [[Perfected Theory]] (1-mana modal) is first cut.
* [[Hexhaven Dueling Arena]] (re-prepare land) costs a land drop for a trick needing a prepared attacker—unplayable; rebuy with [[Unsummon]] instead.
* Overkilling a 1-loyalty Jace when face damage wins faster.
* Emptying the hand into passive white/black—respect sweepers ([[Kindred Judgment]] (white wipe), [[Overwrite the Multiverse]] (black exile wipe), colorless [[The Echoverse Fulcrum]] (any deck)) and keep one rebuild card (flashback/recursion) back.
* Padding with medium 4s/5s or second copies of narrow payoffs over solid curve bodies. [[Theoretical Necromancer]] (4/1 plus yard-return-to-hand) is a fine turn-3 play and better with mill/fat targets—[[Rank Rat]] (1/1 discard) is playable only with a payoff or outlet.
