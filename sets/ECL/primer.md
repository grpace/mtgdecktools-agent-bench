# MTG Lorwyn Eclipsed (ECL) - Limited Deckbuilding Context

## 1. Format Overview and Supported Archetypes

This deck is being built for the **ECL** limited format.

Use these priorities as the baseline when building and tuning a 40-card deck.

### 1.0. Bomb-First Evaluation
* **Start with Rares/Mythics:** ECL is a prince format, so strong rares/mythics often decide games.
* **Include Bombs If Castable:** If your mana can support a high-impact card, prioritize playing it.
* **Synergy Still Matters:** Build a cohesive shell, but break ties in favor of higher-impact cards.

### 1.1. Format Identity
* **Five Main Lanes:** The most supported two-color decks are WU Merfolk, BR Goblins, GW Kithkin, UR Elementals, and BG Elves.
* **Secondary Lanes Exist:** Some color pairs are less supported but still draftable via hybrid cards, changelings, and overlap synergies.
* **Type-Matters Format:** Creature type density matters in most successful decks, especially for behold/count payoffs.

### 1.2. Macro Deckbuilding Rules
* **Play Enough Interaction:** Aim for about six removal spells in most decks.
* **Respect Curve and Board Presence:** Prioritize functional 2-5 mana plays so your synergy cards actually matter.
* **Draft with Flexibility:** Changelings and hybrid cards keep you open and smooth pivots.

## 2. Core Mechanics and Implications

### 2.1. Creature-Type Synergy
* **Deckbuilding Impact:** Many cards reward specific creature types, so density is important.
* **Practical Rule:** Most tribal payoffs improve significantly once you have enough on-type creatures.

### 2.2. Changeling
* **Role:** Changelings are glue cards across archetypes and cross-archetype "party" shells.
* **Deckbuilding Impact:** They improve consistency for behold/count effects and make pivots easier.

### 2.3. Blight (-1/-1 Counter Economy)
* **Role:** Blight functions as a resource trade; you often spend creature toughness for power/tempo.
* **Deckbuilding Impact:** Creatures with higher toughness and counter-management tools gain value.
* **Play Pattern:** Sequence to preserve key creatures while converting blight costs into meaningful advantage.

### 2.4. Hybrid Mana
* **Critical Rule:** Evaluate hybrid pips as **OR**, not AND.
* **Deckbuilding Impact:** A hybrid card is castable if your mana can pay either side of each hybrid symbol.
* **Practical Use:** Recheck powerful hybrid cards before final cuts; many are easier to cast than they look.

## 3. Supported Archetype Baselines

### 3.1. WU Merfolk (Convoke/Tempo)
* **Plan:** Build board presence, leverage convoke, and gain tempo through efficient interaction.
* **Priority:** Cheap creatures that help cast expensive spells ahead of curve.

### 3.2. BR Goblins (Aggro/Pressure)
* **Plan:** Apply early pressure, use removal to keep attacks clean, and finish before opponents stabilize.
* **Priority:** Low curve, reach, and proactive combat turns.

### 3.3. GW Kithkin (Go-Wide)
* **Plan:** Develop a wide board and convert multiple bodies into pressure and value.
* **Priority:** Token makers and payoffs that reward creature count.

### 3.4. UR Elementals (Spells + Big-Spell Synergy)
* **Plan:** Bridge early interaction into high-impact turns with elementals and expensive spells.
* **Priority:** Cards that reward casting higher mana-value spells.

### 3.5. BG Elves (Graveyard/Midrange)
* **Plan:** Grind value via graveyard interaction and efficient board development.
* **Priority:** Cards that convert recursion and attrition into inevitability.

## 4. Practical Build Guidance

* **Build a Real Deck, Not a Theme Pile:** Keep synergy packages tight and avoid low-impact filler.
* **Use Removal on Must-Answer Threats:** Save premium interaction for bombs and snowball engines.
* **Balance Power and Fit:** Include castable bombs, then optimize support cards around your main plan.
* **Avoid Over-Splashing:** Splash only when fixing is real and payoff is high.

## 5. Quick Final Checklist

* Is your primary archetype plan clear?
* Do you have enough early plays and interaction?
* Are your key type/payoff cards supported by enough enablers?
* Did you re-check castable hybrid bombs before final cuts?
* Can your mana base cast your important cards on time?
