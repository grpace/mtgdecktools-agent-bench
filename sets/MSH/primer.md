### **Marvel Super Heroes (MSH) Limited Context**

**MSH supplement only.** The main deck-building prompt already defines your card pool and pool-only rules. Everything below is **Marvel Super Heroes format guidance**—mechanics, lane patterns, and deck shapes. [[Card names]] illustrate typical roles (signpost, enabler, payoff); treat them as examples of *what to look for* in your pool, not as cards to add. **Pick the best plan from your actual pool** (card quality, synergies, mana, interaction)—not from format tier lists or podcast rankings.

### **Format Basics**

* **Mid-range default.** Most winning decks are **mid-range**: favored in **creature combat**, good at **racing** when boards stall, and they **do not run out of mana** late. Aggro and grind are shades of mid-range here—not opposite poles. Avoid “in the middle” lists with no standalone power or late mana sinks.
* **Card quality first.** Prioritize cards that **do good things on their own** at any stage of the game. Finicky synergy hoops lose to opponents casting solid removal, flyers, and lifelink bodies. Synergy makes good cards better—it does not make bad cards good.
* **All ten two-color pairs are supported.** Each pair has one **signpost uncommon** and one **hybrid gold**—20 strong multicolor cards total. Hybrid mana cards can slot into many decks and show up often.
* **High synergy—but rate wins.** Decks can do a specific thing, but loose “synergy piles” often lose to coherent **good cards**. Signposts are hints; do not force a fragile theme when better pool cards point elsewhere.
* **Legendary density.** Every uncommon, rare, and mythic **creature** is legendary—only **commons** are non-legendary. You may run multiples of the same legendary; the legend rule forces a sacrifice when a second copy enters. Keep the copy with counters, Power-up already used, or better board position.
* **Cooking format.** Winning decks in the same colors look different based on build-arounds in your pool (e.g. [[Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards]] wants tokens; without him, token makers drop in priority).
* **Creature types:** **Hero** and **Villain** drive many payoffs—GW wants Heroes, BR can use Villains as a bonus; try to pick one theme per deck, not both. **Robot** (UR, BR) and **Gamma** (RG) show up in several lanes.
* **Color strength (loose):** **White → blue → green → black → red.** White and blue supply the best mid-range tools (interaction, well-statted bodies, flyers, lifelink, mana sinks). Red is weakest—understated creatures, clunky removal, weak Power-up payoffs. Black sits in the middle. Green can anchor fixing-heavy shells.
* **Splashing and three colors.** The further your pool is from a **white and/or blue** core, the more you usually need **splashes** to hit removal, racing tools, and late mana sinks. Fixing is good (gainlands, landcyclers, [[Undercover Skrull]], [[Ant-Man's Army]], treasure). Splash a third color with fixing and clear payoffs (bombs, [[Jessica Jones, Private Eye]], [[Vision Quest]], **or premium removal**). Include **one basic per splashed color** so landcyclers can fetch it.
* **Splash for removal.** Removal is premium—**splashing for answers is a very viable strategy** in this format. Landcyclers ([[Borough Backup]], [[A.I.M. Scientists]], [[Kree Sentinel]], [[Roxxon Brutes]], [[Savage Land Dinosaur]]) make off-color removal easy to cast: cycle on two, fetch the splash basic later. If the pool has splashable premium removal ([[Web Up]], [[Murdock's Crusade]], [[Dark Deed]], [[Cruel Alliance]], [[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[Punishing Punch]], [[Go Nuts!]]) and you have landcyclers or other fixing, **absolutely consider the splash** rather than playing a deck short on answers.
* **Live format notes.** **Bombs are strong**—top rares and mythics often win games; build around, splash for, or protect them when in pool. Early standouts: [[Jennifer Walters]], [[Agent Phil Coulson]], [[The Wondrous Wasp]], [[M.O.D.O.K.]], [[Justice, Vance Astrovik]], [[Avengers: Under Siege]], [[Vision Quest]]—prioritize when the power gap is large even off-signpost. **Removal is very strong** at common and uncommon; pack **5–6 answers** (tappers like [[Raft Security Officer]] count)—splash off-color answers when the main colors are light. **White is deep**—UW, GW, and WB all put up strong results when the pool has white cards.
* **Combat-centric racing.** Boards stall and ground creatures trade; **flying** and **lifelink** win races. Prioritize evasion ([[Falcon, Winged Wonder]], [[Giant-Sized Flying Ant]], [[H.E.R.B.I.E. Scout Unit]], [[Kree Commandos]]) and lifelink ([[Brave Brawler]]) when in pool. Match stats to the format—your three-drop should block their two-drop when possible.
* **4 toughness / 4 mana value** is common. Pair solid reach ([[Lightning Strike]], [[Repulsor Blast]]) with **premium** answers for bigger bodies ([[Murdock's Crusade]], [[Web Up]], [[Dark Deed]], [[Go Nuts!]], fight effects).
* **Atypical curve.** Three- and four-drops are easy to fill; **premium two-drops are scarce**. Landcyclers ([[Borough Backup]], [[A.I.M. Scientists]], [[Kree Sentinel]], [[Roxxon Brutes]], [[Savage Land Dinosaur]]) count as turn-two mana plays—normal pattern is landcycle on two, three-drop on three. Target roughly **~6 real two-drops** plus **2–3 landcyclers**; prefer a strong two over a medium three or four when picks are close. Avoid low-rate twos like [[Agents of HYDRA]] and [[K'un-Lun Warrior]] just to curve; [[Guerrilla Gorilla]] is low-priority filler. Strong twos include [[White Tiger, Ava Ayala]], [[We Say Thee Nay!]], [[Brave Brawler]], [[Raft Security Officer]], and [[Undercover Skrull]].
* **Creature count.** Aim for **15–16 creatures**; below **14** feels light. Cast a creature on two, three, and four when you can. Go-wide shells can reach **17–18** when tokens and landcyclers add bodies.
* **Interaction matters.** **5–6 removal/tempo answers** is the sweet spot. Tempo answers ([[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[Frozen in Ice]], [[Raft Security Officer]]) are fine—clear blockers for a race turn, not only hard exile. **Premium removal:** [[Web Up]], [[Murdock's Crusade]], [[Dark Deed]], [[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[Take Up the Shield]], [[Punishing Punch]], [[Cruel Alliance]]. **Solid removal / reach:** [[Hour of Defeat]], [[Go Nuts!]], [[Frozen in Ice]], [[Super Villain Lockup]], [[Repulsor Blast]], [[Lightning Strike]].
* **Late mana sinks.** Mid-range decks need ways to spend mana after turn four—**non-red Power-up** creatures ([[Bold Biochemist]] draws two), value creatures ([[Hero in Training]], [[Crimson Operative]], [[Jessica Jones, Private Eye]]), and uncommon lands ([[Avengers Tower]], [[Baxter Building]], [[Villainous Hideout]]). **Red Power-up** payoffs ([[Volcanic Villain]], [[Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero]]) underperform unless the pool is already RG-heavy.
* **Land drops matter** more than flooding—mulligan hands that miss early lands; connive decks balance pitching lands vs nonlands. **16–17 lands** plus 2–3 landcyclers is a common sweet spot depending on splash and curve.
* **Expensive legends.** Many 4+ mana legends have no ETB, haste, or protection—they pay off if you **untap with them**. With strong removal everywhere, fragile five-drops need stabilization, protection, or a clear reason to run; **bombs** that stick often decide games.
* **Fixing and splashing.** Gainlands at common ([[Asgardian Citadel]], [[A.I.M. Labs]], [[Stark Industries]], etc.) enter tapped—**only include ones whose both colors match your deck**. Uncommon lands ([[Surveillance Room]], [[Baxter Building]], [[Avengers Tower]], [[Villainous Hideout]]) break board stalls and help splashes. **Basic landcyclers** are the main reason splash-for-removal works—discard on two, fetch a splash basic later when you need the color. When you splash, **run at least one basic land of that splash color** in your deck so cyclers can find it.
* **Equipment.** Prioritize when strong carriers are in the pool: [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Spy Kit]] (cheap pump, untap plus scry on solo attack—excellent in combat and races; run multiple copies when available), [[Captain America's Shield]] (+0/+8 vigilance, taps blockers on attack—strong race card), [[U.S.Agent, John Walker]], [[War Machine, Legacy of Iron]] (only with several power boosts—see traps), [[Ronin, Shadow Stalker]]. [[Hawkeye's Bow]] is fine in RW shells but not a lane reason.

### **Key Mechanics**

1. **Power-up:** Once per creature at **instant speed**; cost reduced by that creature's mana cost if it entered this turn. Best **same turn** when possible. **Non-red Power-up** cards are strong mana sinks early and late ([[Bold Biochemist]], [[Aerial Doombot]]). **Red Power-up** bodies ([[Volcanic Villain]], [[Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero]]) are weaker—treat as filler unless RG is already your best pool. [[Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero]] doubles activations—bonus in RG, not a lane to force.
2. **Teamwork:** Tap creatures with total power N (summoning sickness OK). Modal spells often **choose both modes** with Teamwork. Few per pool—a handful, not a full engine. [[Captain America, Living Legend]] untaps after Teamwork taps; black 2/1 Villain tokens help pay costs. High Teamwork numbers (3–4) are hard on curve—treat as bonus.
3. **Plan:** Enchantments that gain counters from triggers, then sacrifice for a payoff. Most need heavy dedicated support across several turns; cut when setup outruns your pool.
4. **Connive:** Draw, discard, maybe +1/+1 counter—even if the creature dies, you still draw and discard (no counter). Strong for UB draw-two and BG yard filling. [[Moonstone, Harsh Mistress]] replays discarded cards in connive shells. Pairs with [[Misty Knight, Hero for Hire]] and landcyclers for late draws.
5. **Double-faced characters:** Cast the front for less or pay the full back-face cost—**build for the front face.**
6. **Worthy:** Legendary, red and/or white, not a Villain. [[Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor]] checks on equip.
7. **Harness:** [[The Mind Stone]]—build-around; needs support.
8. **Boast:** Villain combat (e.g. [[Baron Helmut Zemo]] in BR).
9. **Shield counters / Sagas / Vehicles:** Shield counters strong in GW; Sagas and Vehicles are minor role players. [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier]] crews well with [[Captain America, Living Legend]].

### **Draft Archetypes**

Each lane lists its **signpost uncommon** first. Commit to the pair whose pool has the most castable gold, synergy, and interaction—but **rate and mid-range tools beat forced signposts**.

* **UW Teamwork — [[Captain America, Living Legend]]**
  * **Plan:** Tempo and flyers; Teamwork and tap-untap synergies when the pool has spells. **White-blue is a premier lane**—strong card quality, removal, flyers, and mana sinks even with only a few Teamwork spells.
  * **Signpost:** [[Captain America, Living Legend]]—3/4 vigilance; untaps creatures you tap for Teamwork or gives pseudo-vigilance when attacking; pairs with [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Helicarrier]] crew. Solid role player, not the main reason UW wins.
  * **Standouts:** [[Justice, Vance Astrovik]] (elite gold payoff), [[Agent Maria Hill]] (draws when tapped for Teamwork—excellent one-drop when you have any Teamwork; run multiples if in pool), [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Deployment Drone]] (top blue common—flying artifact body plus connive), [[Giant-Sized Flying Ant]] (flash flyer; tap or untap permanents—overperforms in races).
  * **Payoffs:** [[Okoye, Dora Milaje Leader]] (two 1/1s, token first strike). [[Borough Backup]] (two 3/2 Hero tokens plus landcycling).
  * **Support:** [[Falcon, Winged Wonder]] (surveil on attack breaks stalls), [[Atlantis Attacks]], [[We Say Thee Nay!]] (premium two-drop counter), [[Helicarrier Strike]], [[Kree Commandos]] (flying prowess), [[Raft Security Officer]] (tap interaction that scales—counts toward removal target). [[H.E.R.B.I.E. Scout Unit]] is a strong four-drop flyer and two-for-one in go-wide flyer shells.

* **UB Draw-two — [[Kang, Temporal Tyrant]]**
  * **Plan:** **Draw two cards in a turn**—core connive lane; midrange grind. [[M.O.D.O.K.]] is a format bomb when you can cast it—build UB around it if it is in your pool.
  * **Signpost:** [[Kang, Temporal Tyrant]] connives on attack and drains on second draw—even without attacking if other draw triggers fire. Average on its own; connive shell and bombs carry the lane.
  * **Enablers:** [[Madame Masque]], [[Trickster's Stratagem]] (excellent—second from top plus connive), [[Kid Loki]], [[Scientist Supreme of A.I.M.]], [[Pym Particles]], [[Bold Biochemist]] (Power-up draws two—strong mana sink).
  * **Support:** [[Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards]] with token makers ([[S.H.I.E.L.D. Deployment Drone]], [[Borough Backup]]). Fantastic Four cards ([[Human Torch, Johnny Storm]], [[The Thing, Ben Grimm]], [[Invisible Woman, Sue Storm]], [[Mister Fantastic, Reed Richards]])—play each on rate; do not force the full loop, especially [[Invisible Woman, Sue Storm]] without heavy support. [[Echo, Perceptive Prodigy]] copies creature triggers with little setup. [[Frozen in Ice]] taps down legends—often forces awkward legend-rule sacrifices.

* **BR Villains — [[Madame Hydra]]**
  * **Plan:** **Black-red midrange**—removal and solid bodies when actually in pool. **Do not force the intended Villain engine** when better cards in other colors are open; splash choice red/black removal and payoffs ([[Jessica Jones, Private Eye]], [[Cruel Alliance]], [[Dark Deed]]) into a stronger base (often green or white) instead of a fragile BR Villain pile.
  * **Signpost:** [[Madame Hydra]]—2/1 Villain token with menace on each Villain spell. Rate-only unless you already have a strong BR shell; not a build-around.
  * **Enablers:** [[HYDRA Troopers]] (needs two creatures in yard—mill two sets up the next copy; weak alone on turn three). [[Baron Strucker, HYDRA Overlord]], [[Arnim Zola, Bio-Fanatic]].
  * **Support:** [[Cruel Alliance]], [[Dark Deed]], [[Evil's Thrall]], [[HYDRA Assault Robot]]. [[The Masters of Evil]], [[Crossbones, Malicious Mercenary]], and [[Hire a Crew]] are fine on rate, not reasons to be BR. Avoid building around [[Project Deathlok Soldier]]—needs too much setup for this format.

* **RG Power-up stompy — [[Hulk, Gamma Goliath]]**
  * **Plan:** Big bodies and **Power-up**—beefy, aggressive. Punches through 4 toughness. Lean on **non-red** Power-up enablers in the pool when possible; red Power-up bodies are often filler.
  * **Signpost:** [[Hulk, Gamma Goliath]] discounts other Power-ups by {3}—powered-up Hulk ends games.
  * **Engine:** [[Abomination, Terrifying Titan]]. [[Wonder Man, Hollywood Hero]] is extra Power-up value if already in RG—not a reason to draft the lane. [[Red Hulk]] plus [[Photon Blast Barrage]] for enrage bursts—fine, not premium.
  * **Standouts:** [[She-Hulk, Jade Defender]], [[Jennifer Walters]] (bomb—prioritize even off-theme), [[White Tiger, Ava Ayala]], [[Hercules, Prince of Power]], [[Brave Brawler]] (lifelink two-drop that grows—format staple). [[Go Nuts!]]—fight first, or Teamwork 3 for counter then fight (need 3 power on board before casting—counter does not buff the target for Teamwork).

* **GW Heroes — [[Black Panther, Vanguard]]**
  * **Plan:** **Hero** go-wide and tokens—Heroes are common; do not force every pick. **Green-white is strong** when hero payoffs and white removal line up in the pool.
  * **Signpost:** [[Black Panther, Vanguard]]—1/1 or team buff on each non-token Hero.
  * **Rate cards:** [[Hero in Training]] (top common in the format—draws a card, two life with another Hero; run in almost any white deck), [[Political Triumph]] (strong white uncommon), [[Ka-Zar of the Savage Land]], [[Borough Backup]], [[Wakandan Royal Guard]], [[Captain America, Super-Soldier]] (shield counters).
  * **Support:** [[Okoye, Dora Milaje Leader]], [[Avengers Tower]] (dig Heroes in stalls). [[The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl]] is a premier finisher if the pool supports triple green. [[Rapid Rescue]] (one copy—see Deck Shapes) when the list is permanent-heavy.

* **WB Attack alone — [[Black Widow, Double Agent]]**
  * **Plan:** **Attack alone** payoffs; Equipment and tricks help solo attackers. **White-black is viable** when solo-attack payoffs and white removal stack—white card quality carries the deck.
  * **Signpost:** [[Black Widow, Double Agent]]—solo attackers get first strike and menace; alone she is first strike, menace, deathtouch.
  * **Enablers:** [[Agent 13, Sharon Carter]], [[Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.]] (+1/+1 on solo attackers), [[Luke Cage, Power Man]], [[U.S.Agent, John Walker]], [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Spy Kit]] (untap plus scry on solo attacks—premium Equipment).
  * **Support:** [[Political Triumph]], [[HYDRA Infiltration]], [[Stolen Stark Tech]] (pump plus Equipment stickiness), [[Captain America's Shield]] (vigilance race finisher on a solo attacker).

* **BG Graveyard — [[Killmonger, Scourge of Wakanda]]**
  * **Plan:** **Two+ creature cards in yard**—connive and trades fill naturally; black removal covers green's thinner interaction. Needs real payoffs like the signpost—not just [[HYDRA Troopers]] alone.
  * **Signpost:** [[Killmonger, Scourge of Wakanda]]—sacrifice on entry to remove a permanent; 5/4 with threshold. One of the better gold payoffs—worth building toward.
  * **Support:** [[Rapid Rescue]] turn one, landcycle turn two, [[HYDRA Troopers]] turn three is the ideal setup line. [[Klaw, Sonic Subjugator]], [[Punishing Punch]] ({1} with threshold), [[Undercover Skrull]] (ramp once yard fills). Keep **~35 permanents** if running [[Rapid Rescue]]—limit instants and narrow tricks.

* **GU Counters — [[Ant-Man, Colony Commander]]**
  * **Plan:** +1/+1 counters **and** ramp—commit only when counter enablers and payoffs stack in the pool. Weak signpost early; do not force the lane for the signpost alone.
  * **Signpost:** [[Ant-Man, Colony Commander]]—needs other counter sources each turn.
  * **Engine:** [[Doc Samson, Super Psychiatrist]], [[Knight of Wundagore]], [[Savage Land Dinosaur]] (big body plus landcycling), [[Mister Hyde, Monster Within]], [[Kid Loki]]. [[Hulkling, Burgeoning Bruiser]] feeds counters into [[Mister Hyde, Monster Within]] for draw loops when both are in pool. [[Ant-Man's Army]] and [[Undercover Skrull]] help splash bombs and interaction.

* **UR Artifacts — [[Iron Man, Master of Machines]]**
  * **Plan:** **Artifact** count—Robots, Equipment, tokens, colorless artifacts like [[H.E.R.B.I.E. Scout Unit]]. Aim for ~8–10+ **playable** artifact sources; quality over filler. [[Vision Quest]] is a premier bomb—splashable; only **2–3 artifact creatures** needed because it searches library **or** graveyard.
  * **Signpost:** [[Iron Man, Master of Machines]]—play an artifact before combat when possible; strong with [[Vision Quest]] (artifact ETB triggers draw on attack).
  * **Bodies:** [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Deployment Drone]], [[Iron Lad, Diverging Destiny]], [[Aerial Doombot]], [[Futurist Forge]], [[H.E.R.B.I.E. Scout Unit]], [[Crimson Operative]], [[Machinesmith Automaton]], [[HYDRA Assault Robot]], [[The Vision]] (Power-up plus draw on big attacks after Quest).

* **RW Noncreature spells — [[Thor Odinson]]**
  * **Plan:** Spells-matter tempo when the pool has cheap interaction and bodies—not a premier lane early. Avoid forcing [[Iron Fist, Living Weapon]] or [[Thor Odinson]] as the deck engine.
  * **Signpost:** [[Thor Odinson]]—double **prowess**; must untap with a five-mana legend. Fragile and underperforming as a build-around.
  * **Spells & tricks:** [[Take Up the Shield]], [[Loki Laufeyson]] (copies cheap instants like [[Lightning Strike]]—play before powering him up), [[Lightning Strike]] (solid reach). [[Blazing Crescendo]] and [[Repulsor Blast]] are fine fillers. [[Hawkeye's Bow]] (reach, cheap equip). [[Hawkeye, Young Avenger]] plus ping spells ([[Photon Blast Barrage]], [[Lightning Strike]]) is a niche shell—fragile without protection; play on rate, not as a default plan.

**Overlap:** Villains + connive (BR/UB). Graveyard + connive (BG). Teamwork + tokens (UW/GW). Splash third color when fixing and a bomb **or premium removal** align. Any lane wins when the pool has the best mix of **standalone power**, synergy, and mana.

### **Deck Shapes (Speed & Synergy)**

* **Default: mid-range.** Build a creature-combat deck that can race with flyers/lifelink and still spend mana on turns six-plus. Pick the pool's best mix of rate, removal, and mana sinks—not a signpost from a tier list.
* **Pool first:** Compare viable strategies from card quality and synergies—build the strongest 40.
* **Composition targets:** **15–16 creatures**, **5–6 interaction** (removal plus tappers), **~6 two-drops** (plus landcyclers), **16–17 lands** with fixing when splashing. **8-ish noncreatures**—most are removal and combat helpers; do not overload on draw spells that cut creatures.
* **Slots 1–18 vs 19–23:** Fill early slots with **standalone good cards**. Synergy-dependent bodies ([[War Machine, Legacy of Iron]], [[Speedball, New Warrior]], [[Spider-Man, To the Rescue]]) belong in **last few slots** only when the pool has enough pumps or enablers (e.g. [[S.H.I.E.L.D. Spy Kit]]).
* **Splashing:** Strong W/U base → minimal splash unless you need more answers. G/R/B-heavy pool → expect **three colors** with [[Undercover Skrull]], [[Ant-Man's Army]], landcyclers, and gainlands. **Splash for removal first** when short of 5–6 answers—off-color [[Web Up]], [[Murdock's Crusade]], [[Dark Deed]], [[Cruel Alliance]], [[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[Punishing Punch]], or [[Go Nuts!]] with landcyclers plus one splash basic is a standard, high-value plan. Also splash **choice** red/black cards ([[Jessica Jones, Private Eye]], [[Crimson Operative]]) into green or white shells rather than forcing BR Villains.
* **Good red cards (splash picks):** [[Jessica Jones, Private Eye]] (card advantage, must-answer), [[Crimson Operative]] (exile-cast—red [[Hero in Training]]), [[Lightning Strike]], [[Photon Blast Barrage]], [[Misty Knight, Hero for Hire]] (pairs with landcyclers), [[Loki Laufeyson]] with multiple cheap instants. Most other red commons are filler or clunky removal ([[Repulsor Blast]] only when you need a sixth answer).
* **Tempo/aggro shades (WR, RW, RG, parts of WB):** Pressure by turn four; [[Take Up the Shield]], [[Helicarrier Strike]], [[Lightning Strike]], [[Blazing Crescendo]], [[Captain America's Shield]]. Still keep 5–6 answers and late mana sinks.
* **Value/grind shades (UB, BR, BG, parts of UW):** Premium removal and connive first, then engines—do not spend early turns only setting up if you lack survival. Connive needs nonlands to pitch.
* **UR artifacts / GU ramp:** Playable only when the pool delivers real density without filler creatures eating slots. [[Vision Quest]] alone can justify light artifact count.
* **Premium removal (when in pool):** [[Web Up]] (exile any nonland), [[Murdock's Crusade]] (exile toughness 4+), [[Dark Deed]] (-4/-4), [[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[Take Up the Shield]], [[Punishing Punch]], [[Cruel Alliance]] (exile MV ≤3, or any creature with Teamwork 2 plus life), [[Go Nuts!]], [[Frozen in Ice]], [[Super Villain Lockup]] (exile tapped creature—misses vigilance bodies).
* **Top commons:** [[Hero in Training]] is among the best commons in the format—two mana, draws a card, two life with another Hero, and slots into UW, GW, WB, and any white splash. **Strong commons by color:** White—[[Hero in Training]], [[Web Up]], [[Take Up the Shield]], [[Borough Backup]], [[Brave Brawler]], [[Political Triumph]], [[Raft Security Officer]]. Blue—[[S.H.I.E.L.D. Deployment Drone]], [[Trickster's Stratagem]], [[We Say Thee Nay!]], [[Giant-Sized Flying Ant]]. Black—[[Cruel Alliance]], [[Dark Deed]], [[HYDRA Troopers]] (needs yard setup). Red—[[Lightning Strike]], [[Photon Blast Barrage]] (splash); [[Repulsor Blast]] as filler removal. Green—[[Go Nuts!]], [[Rapid Rescue]] (one copy), [[Savage Land Dinosaur]], [[Undercover Skrull]].
* **Bombs:** When a rare or mythic in your pool is clearly powerful and fits your mana, treat it as a build-around or splash target. Early standouts: [[Jennifer Walters]], [[Agent Phil Coulson]], [[The Wondrous Wasp]], [[M.O.D.O.K.]], [[Justice, Vance Astrovik]], [[Avengers: Under Siege]], [[Killmonger, Scourge of Wakanda]], [[Vision Quest]]—prioritize over marginal synergy when the power gap is large.
* **[[Rapid Rescue]]:** Strong one-mana selection plus two life when the deck has **~35 permanents** (limit instants and narrow tricks). **First copy** only—the second copy gets worse.
* **Mana-sync lands:** [[Avengers Tower]] (best rate—happy to splash for), [[Baxter Building]] (filters plus draw with 4+ toughness), [[Villainous Hideout]]—all strong in mid-range stalls.
* **Colorless glue:** [[H.E.R.B.I.E. Scout Unit]] (draw plus land drop)—strong in many decks; competes in the four-drop slot but high floor. [[A.I.M. Synthoids]] is curve filler only.
* **Mana base:** Gainlands plus landcyclers; [[Surveillance Room]], [[Baxter Building]], [[Avengers Tower]], [[Dependable Quinjet]], [[Restorative Technique]]. **Splashing:** include one basic of each splashed color so landcyclers can fetch it—do not rely only on tapped duals in hand. Save treasure tokens for splashes.
* **Breaking stalls:** **Flying** is the most reliable finisher—ground boards stall often. Also lifelink ([[Brave Brawler]]), trample, card selection ([[Falcon, Winged Wonder]] surveil), and grind engines—pick what your pool actually has.

### **Common Mistakes**

* Picking a lane from a tier list instead of the pool's best mix of card quality, synergy, and mana.
* **Too mid-range with no plan**—a pile of medium cards with no removal, racing tools, or late mana sinks.
* Skimping on removal when the pool has strong answers—target **5–6** interaction pieces; if main colors are short, **splash** with landcyclers rather than cutting interaction.
* Passing on splashable premium removal because "we are two-color"—landcyclers make splash-for-removal one of the best plans in the format.
* Undervaluing a **bomb** in the pool because it does not fit a signpost lane—splash or pivot when the bomb justifies it ([[Vision Quest]] off-theme is often correct).
* Forcing **bad synergy cards** without support—drawing them alone is a bad card; synergy makes good cards better, not bad cards good.
* Running **[[Project Deathlok Soldier]]**, **[[Kingpin's Enforcers]]** loops, or other graveyard hoops when the pool has standalone playables instead.
* Playing **[[War Machine, Legacy of Iron]]** or **[[Speedball, New Warrior]]** in main slots without enough power boosts—reserve for slots 19–23 or cut.
* **Second copy of [[Rapid Rescue]]**—the first copy needs ~35 permanents; additional copies get worse.
* BG sacrificing tokens expecting yard payoffs (tokens don't stay in the yard).
* Plans without enough enabler actions across several turns.
* Missing land drops; don't connive away all development by pitching every land.
* Burning removal on medium bodies while the real engine survives.
* **Only** [[Lightning Strike]] for removal when the pool lacks answers for 4+ toughness—keep the reach, add premium bigger answers like [[Murdock's Crusade]] or [[Web Up]].
* UR artifacts: filler artifacts for count, or cutting good artifacts for non-theme creatures.
* Forcing **BR Villains** around [[Madame Hydra]] when splashing black/red good cards into another base is stronger.
* Building around **Fantastic Four** as a four-card loop—play pieces on rate, do not assemble the full package.
