## MTG Deck Tools deckbuilding guide

_Condensed from the rules behind the MTG Deck Tools AI deck builder (mtgdecktools.com). Where the set primer disagrees (format speed, land count, curve), the set primer wins._

**1. Compare before you commit.** Sketch 2–3 real options (different colour pairs or plans) from your pool and compare them on raw power, synergy density, mana consistency, curve, and interaction. Don't take the first coherent deck you see; take the best one.

**2. Curve and consistency.**
- Aim for at least 5–6 proactive plays at mana value 2. A deck that can't act on turns 2–4 loses to tempo.
- 16–17 lands for 40 cards (16 for low curves, 17 normally, 18 only for very top-heavy decks or heavy mana sinks).
- Usually 14–17 creatures; a deck needs bodies to block and to pressure.
- Higher raw power with a broken curve is usually worse than slightly lower power that casts its spells on time.

**3. The rule of one ("clunk factor").** A deck survives one structural flaw (weak curve OR shaky mana OR thin creature count), not two. If your curve is already weak, don't splash.

**4. Splashing (strict land math).**
- 1 splashed card → at least 3 sources of that colour; 2 cards → 4 sources; 3 cards → 5–6 sources. Sources include basics, on-colour duals and fetch effects (with at least one basic of that colour to fetch).
- Never splash mana value 1–2 cards (even premium removal) unless it is a true game-winning bomb; you won't have the splash on turns 1–2.
- Avoid splashing double-pipped costs. Aggressive decks should limit enters-tapped lands; slower decks can afford them.
- Exception: if your main colours lack any way to win, a greedy splash for real power can be correct.

**5. Mana colours and lands.**
- Your deck's colours come from the hard coloured pips of the spells you play.
- Hybrid symbols are OR, not AND: a {R/G} card is castable in any deck with red OR green and never forces the other colour into your mana.
- Only play a dual land if you need both colours it makes; an off-colour dual is worse than a basic. Never add off-colour basics "just in case".
- Check both faces of double-faced/prepare/adventure cards, flashback and activated-ability costs, but a card belongs if its main face is castable.

**6. Interaction and closing power.**
- Count your removal and other interaction (bounce, counters, fight effects). Can you answer the format's common threats and bombs?
- Balance threats and answers: all threats loses to removal-heavy decks, all answers can't close.
- Include parity breakers to win stalled boards: evasion (flying, menace, unblockable), trample, or late-game mana sinks.
- Prefer some instant-speed interaction, and early interaction if the format is fast.

**7. Final check.** Count: lands, creatures, two-drops, removal, colour sources per colour. Every card in the 40 should be castable with your mana and pull its weight in your plan.
