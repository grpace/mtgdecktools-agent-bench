# Launch Checklist

One list of what must be true before Agent Bench opens to the public, grouped by area. Keep it current: when an item changes, update its row, and note it in STATUS.md.

- **Status:** Done, Partial or Missing.
- **Owner:** **Us** (the project's agents and the lead) or **Owner** (Greg: accounts, legal, launch calls).
- **When:** **Before** launch (must-fix), **Soon** after (first weeks), or **Later**.
- **Effort:** S (under 2 hours), M (under a day), L (more).
- Evidence cites `file:line` at the time of the audit (2026-10-22, commit `5f1122a` plus uncommitted security work); line numbers drift.

_Last reviewed: 2026-10-22 (D, E-2 and H rows updated later that day; C-2, E-9, F-3, F-4 and Owner Decisions 5–6 that evening); 2026-10-23: the critic review's data and methodology items (A-1, A-4, A-6, A-11–A-17, F-7, F-9, F-10, Decision 7; reports in `../mtgdecktools-agent-bench-audits/critic-2026-10-22/`); later that day A-6 done (calibration rerun, the set component), A-18, prompts v7, H-8 (network separation under Coolify)._

**Go-live runbook:** [LAUNCH-DAY.md](LAUNCH-DAY.md), every launch-day step in order (owner and us), with commands, checks and rollback. Production is a separate stack (`INSTANCE=prod deploy/deploy.sh`) that starts on fresh data.
